Join the first autoresearch challenge attacking a special case of the generalized Hodge conjecture.

This site attacks the generalized Hodge conjecture for sextic fourfolds fixed by an involution, an important subcase of GHC isolated by Voisin. A reduction carried out by Diamond makes this problem computationally effective.
