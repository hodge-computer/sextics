1. POSITIVE REPAIR: A GENERAL-CUBIC MOVING-POINT SOURCE

The earlier explicit current covered w^3=-P(t). Our exact quotient has both
w^2 and w terms. GeneralCubicCurrent.lean now proves the polynomial algebra
needed for that general case.

Write
  G=w^3+a(x)w^2+b(x)w+c(x),  x=t^2,  g=G_w,
where deg(a)<=1, deg(b)<=2, deg(c)<=3. At an actual moving point (alpha,beta),
G(alpha,beta)=0, define
  H=w^2+(beta+a(alpha^2))*w+beta^2+a(alpha^2)*beta+b(alpha^2).
The current kernel is
  (H/g) * (dlog(t-alpha)-dlog(t+alpha)).
For a closed input eta, its differentiated source is
  2*(M0+M1*w+M2*w^2)/g^3 * dt wedge dr wedge ds.

The checked algebra proves the numerator reduction modulo G, the automatic
relation
  3*M0-a*M1+b*M2=0,
and vanishing of the reduced numerator at the moving point. The latter is
the algebraic core of cancellation of the apparent t^2-alpha^2 denominator.
It also proves the exact normal-source identity
  g*d_w(Rstar)-G_ww*Rstar = 2*(M0+M1*w+M2*w^2),
  Rstar=-M1/3-(2*M2/3)*w.
No cubic-discriminant denominator is introduced by this conversion.

2. DIRECT BINDING TO THE PROTECTED F, A AND JACOBIAN

SymmetricCubicModel.lean evaluates the unchanged generated HodgeSubmission
polynomials at
  X=(1+v,1-v,r), Y=(z+lambda*v,z-lambda*v,s*z),
and proves
  eval(F)=G_raw(r,s,lambda,z^2,v^2),
  eval(A)=r*s*z*(1-v^2)*(z^2-lambda^2*v^2).

It proves the full cubic expansion and its leading coefficient
  ell=(14*lambda^6-10*lambda^4-5*lambda^2+14)/7,
and the monic normalization when ell!=0. It also proves ell(0)=2.
The scaled cone formulas have the correct scale^6 factors.

The polynomial coordinate map with input order
  (scale,r,z,s,v,lambda)
and output order
  (X0,X1,X2,Y0,Y1,Y2)
has its actual partial-derivative matrix formally identified, followed by
  det(J)=+4*scale^5*v*z.

Thus the model and determinant are tied to the actual target; they are not
independently supplied formulas assumed to be the target.

After w=v^2, x=z^2 and monic normalization, put
  C=2*r*s*x/ell^2.
The protected numerator on the chosen residue chart is
  Rstar_target=C*(1-w)*(x-lambda^2*w).
Subtracting (C*lambda^2/3)*g, which has zero normal source, gives a linear
numerator. The remaining normalized traces are exactly

  <M1>=-C*(3*x-lambda^2*b(x)),
  <M2>=(C/2)*(3*x+3*lambda^2+2*lambda^2*a(x)).

The source bounds deg_x(M1)<=4 and deg_x(M2)<=3 leave at most nine coefficient
functions to match. They are rational-function identities in the remaining
base parameters, not merely nine numerical equations. Arbitrary base-
dependent coefficients multiplying closed input forms can destroy closedness.
No such trace equality has been found or assumed.

3. A COMPLETE LEAN LOWER BOUND FOR ALL CERTIFICATE DENOMINATORS

SmallDenominatorObstruction.lean proves
  protected_verified_denominator_degree_ge_four.
It accepts the exact unrestricted record
  HodgeComputer.Verify HodgeSubmission.F HodgeSubmission.A
    HodgeSubmission.F.totalDegree HodgeSubmission.q D N Q
and concludes
  4 <= D.totalDegree.

There is no symmetry restriction, no restriction to rational coefficients,
no assumed normalization, and no requirement that the residual Q vanish.

The proof first normalizes the first equation using denominator legality,
homogeneous projection, and Euler's identity. For degree d<=3, the resulting
linear map (N,D) -> sum N_i*(partial_i(7F))-A*D has a symbolic square
coefficient minor which is diagonal modulo 5, with entries 2 and -1.
The selected derivative monomials contain a fifth power; every A*D monomial
has each exponent at most four. The columns are therefore disjoint and the
minor is nonzero over every characteristic-zero field. Normalization then
forces D=0, contradicting Verify.

This is a theorem about the actual full verifier, not only a failed search.

4. EXACT CAS EXCLUSION OF THE SMALL SYMMETRIC DENOMINATOR FAMILY

Independent exact calculations prove:

  No legal homogeneous S3(X) x S3(Y)-invariant, Y-even denominator
  of degree at most 8 can certify the protected target over C.

This stronger, symmetry-restricted conclusion is NOT a Lean theorem yet.
It is an audited exact linear-algebra/ideal-membership computation, valid
for complex and algebraic coefficients. It is not an enumeration of Fp points.

The complete equivariant vector-field bases use
  E0=sum d/dXi, E1=sum Xi*d/dXi, E2=sum Xi^2*d/dXi
on each block. In elementary symmetric coordinates their true ambient
fields and divergences are retained. The basis is complete and free over
the invariant ring; the scope notes give the elementary proof.

Degree 6: after the exact first equation and the harmless F-gauge, there are
three denominator parameters and ten numerator-kernel parameters. All
kernel corrections are retained. Exact row combinations of the second
equation give a^2=b^2=c^2=0. The three explicit rational row witnesses were
independently checked.

Degree 8: the first matrix has 324 numerator columns, 64 denominator columns,
322 rows, and rank 318. Its full 70-dimensional kernel comprises 48 pure
numerator directions, 18 denominator directions modulo F, and four
polynomial Euler gauges. The second matrix is 435 by 1035. Eliminating all
mixed numerator-kernel terms yields 44 necessary homogeneous quadrics in
18 denominator parameters.

The degree-four Macaulay matrix for these quadrics is 7524 by 5985. It has
full column rank modulo 101. A separately implemented, transposed construction
with variable and row order changes has full row rank modulo 103. Every
rational denominator is a unit at the respective prime. A nonzero maximal
minor therefore lifts to Q, so all quartic monomials lie in the rational
ideal. Every complex common zero has all 18 parameters zero; the resulting
D is a multiple of F and is illegal.

The independent semantic auditor reconstructs the first equation, all kernel
vectors, true ambient divergences, the raw second matrix, and all 44 quadrics
without importing the generator's polynomial routines.

For lower degrees in this symmetry class, multiply a putative certificate by
p^(8-d), p=X0+X1+X2. This preserves the identities and legality: p is prime
and does not divide F, as the point X=(1,-1,0),Y=0 shows. This reduces every
such lower degree to the excluded degree-eight case.

Files and full scope are in research_workaround/independent_degree8_scope.txt.
These results leave larger or noninvariant supports open. They do not refute
the claimed existence theorem.
