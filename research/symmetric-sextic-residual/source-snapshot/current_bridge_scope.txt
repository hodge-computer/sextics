POSITIVE CURRENT, INCIDENCE, AND VERIFIER BRIDGES
==============================================

These files prove reusable construction steps. They do not assert that the
protected target has a certificate. No contact seed, source-matching trace,
legal denominator, or six target numerators are supplied by these modules.

1. CURRENTVERIFIERBRIDGE.LEAN
---------------------------
PolynomialCoordinates records an injective embedding of the actual complex
ambient polynomial ring into a field, together with coordinate derivations
whose restriction is the polynomial partial derivative. Quotient action,
quotient divergence, and the cleared defect identity are proved from these
compatibilities. These are semantic identities for actual polynomials and
actual field derivations, rather than matrices named as formal maps.

For v=N/D and the verifier's polynomial defect,

  embed(defect(F,A,D,q,N))
    = embed(D)^2 * (embed(A)-q*v(embed(F))+embed(F)*div(v)).

verify_of_field_residual builds an actual unchanged Verify record when the
field defect is embed(F)^(q+1)*r and the computed remainder clears as
embed(Q)=embed(D)^2*r. It permits an arbitrary nonzero quotient Q. The
strict variant verify_of_field_game takes the field defect to be zero.

The higher-level residue adapters do not assume the desired polynomial
directional or divergence equations. They use the repository's explicit
bracket correction

  W = [n,T] + div(n) T - (div(T)+A)n/2,   v=-W,

with commuting coordinate derivatives and n(F)=1. The exact error formula
proved in residueCorrection_game_error is

  K_A(v) = -(n(E)+E div(n))/2,
  E = F div(T)-2T(F)-AF.

Thus exact primitive equality E=0 supplies a strict certificate after legal
homogeneous clearing. More generally, if E=F^3 R, the proved formula is

  K_A(v) = F^2 * r,
  r = -(3R+F(n(R)+R div(n)))/2.

The theorem verify_of_residue_primitive_mod_cube converts this weaker
primitive condition into an ordinary Verify F A 6 1 D N Q record, with
the explicit additional clearing identity embed(Q)=embed(D)^2*r.
It does not silently impose a zero remainder or strictness.

REMAINDER LEGALITY: in the full rational function field, E=F^3 R alone is
vacuous, since any E can be divided by F^3. It is not interpreted as a
modulo-F^3 assertion without a regularity condition. The ordinary verifier
adapter explicitly requires the polynomial identity embed(Q)=embed(D)^2*r
for the COMPUTED remainder r, together with IsRelPrime F D. These force r
to be regular along F and supply precisely the required ordinary quotient.
The adapter does not silently infer this identity from the field factorization.
It need not require R itself to be regular if its poles cancel in r; this
avoids imposing a stronger condition than the certificate needs.

The affine adapter uses the existing HomogeneousChart rules to transport a
five-coordinate residue primitive into six homogeneous coordinates. The
actual polynomial/source bindings embed(F)=a^6*f(H), embed(A)=a^6*f(B),
and the equality of the cleared N/D field with the transported correction
are explicit hypotheses. The exact transport identity also proved is

  K_hom(homogenized p) = a^6*f(K_aff(p)),

which retains nonzero remainders. The strict affine endpoint is
verify_of_affine_residue_primitive. Its result is an actual Verify record;
the existing Verify.hasCertificate method can then provide HasCertificate.

What must still be constructed for the protected target:

* The correct finite-cover/current datum and its source identity or
  source identity modulo the hypersurface cube.
* The compatible derivatives, commuting normal/current coordinates,
  and concrete chart/source bindings.
* A common polynomial denominator and numerators representing the actual
  corrected field, with proved homogeneity and coprimality to F.
* For a nonzero remainder, its explicit polynomial quotient clearing.

The modules do not assume a HasCertificate hypothesis to supply these data.
The low-level field-game helper does take a field game identity; the residue
endpoints derive that identity from the displayed primitive relation.

2. CERTIFICATEDENOMINATORGAUGE.LEAN
---------------------------------
The actual ordinary Verify record and an exact normalized first equation
N(F)=AD are the input to a certificate transformation, not an existence
theorem. Let F,A have degree six, D degree b+6, and B homogeneous degree b.
Define

  D'=D+BF,
  N'=N+(AB/6)epsilon,
  Q'=Q+B div(N)-N(B)+AB^2.

The theorem verify_shift proves Verify F A 6 1 D' N' Q'. The old quotient Q
may be nonzero. It proves the degree balance, the new denominator's
coprimality, and its nonvanishing. It does not require those properties of
D' as new assumptions. The proof uses the exact identities

  N'(F)=AD',
  B_D'(N')=B_D(N)+F(B div(N)-N(B)+AB^2).

The nonzero-denominator argument uses the fact that a positive homogeneous
sextic cannot be a unit: evaluation at the origin sends it to zero. This
avoids importing additional degree-theory infrastructure.

3. CONTACTINCIDENCEALGEBRA.LEAN
------------------------------
For derivative symbols of q=h=0 in coordinates (K,alpha,r,s), let

  J=qK*h_alpha-q_alpha*hK,
  u=q_alpha*hr-h_alpha*qr,
  v=q_alpha*hs-h_alpha*qs,
  z=qr*hs-qs*hr,
  ar=hK*qr-qK*hr,
  as=hK*qs-qK*hs.

The file proves u*as-v*ar=J*z and the q/h annihilator identities over every
commutative ring. Over a field with J nonzero, the actual Cramer ratios
(u/J, ar/J) solve the differentiated incidence equations, and the wedge
coefficient is z/J. The nonzero Jacobian and the existence of an incidence
point are not asserted. These identities can be instantiated by the contact
and residue-form modules without a coefficient search.
