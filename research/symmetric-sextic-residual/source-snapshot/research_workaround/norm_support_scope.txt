EXACT AUDITS AND SUPPORT OBSTRUCTIONS
===================================

Protected target
----------------
Write p,q,r for the three elementary symmetric polynomials in X0,X1,X2,
and P,Q,R for those in Y0,Y1,Y2. Their weights are 1,2,3,1,2,3.
Let U=sum Xi^6 and V=sum Yi^6. Throughout,

  F = U+V+(5/7)q^2 Q+(10/7)q Q^2,  A=rR,  pole parameter=1.

G=7F is used only to keep coefficients integral. The normalized equations
for G are N(G)=AD and B_D(N)=D div(N)-N(D) in (G). They are equivalent
to those for F by multiplying N by 7. Block exchange is not a symmetry.

No certificate or rational primitive was found in this investigation.
The results below are obstructions and independent audits. Computational
receipts are not asserted to be Lean proofs. The separate Lean module
SmallDenominatorObstruction proves the low-degree obstruction and its adapter
to the actual unrestricted HodgeComputer.Verify definition.

1. COMPLETE EQUIVARIANT MODULE, NOT A RESTRICTED VECTOR-FIELD ANSATZ
----------------------------------------------------------------
For one block let E_k=sum Xi^k d/dXi, k=0,1,2. An S3-equivariant
polynomial triple is uniquely f0 E_0+f1 E_1+f2 E_2, with invariant
polynomial coefficients fi. Indeed, the first component is invariant under
the stabilizer exchanging X1 and X2, so it is a polynomial in
X0, X1+X2, X1X2. Substitute X1+X2=p-X0 and X1X2=q-p X0+X0^2,
and reduce by the MONIC relation X0^3-p X0^2+q X0-r=0. This gives
f0+f1 X0+f2 X0^2 with polynomial invariant coefficients. Equivariance
gives the same fi for all three components. Uniqueness follows from the
nonzero Vandermonde determinant, and hence holds in the polynomial ring.
This reasoning also applies with the other block's invariant ring as
coefficient ring. No discriminant denominator is introduced.

In elementary coordinates the fields and their AMBIENT divergences are

  E0 = (3,2p,q),                 div=0;
  E1 = (p,2q,3r),                div=3;
  E2 = (p^2-2q,pq-3r,pr),        div=2p.

The divergence of f E_k is E_k(f)+f div(E_k). The auditor additionally
checks each supplied field's divergence using the independent identity

  2 Delta (sum_j d(v_j)/d(s_j) - div_ambient(v)) = v(Delta),

where Delta=disc(p,q,r)disc(P,Q,R) is the square of the Jacobian of
the elementary-symmetric coordinate map.

If D is invariant under S3(X) x S3(Y), averaging N preserves the same D
and both normalized equations. Averaging under Y -> -Y likewise gives
the prescribed parity when D is Y-even and A is Y-odd. At denominator
degree six, numerator degree seven, the coefficient of E_k has weighted
degree 7-k. X-block coefficients have odd Y-degree; Y-block coefficients
have parity k modulo two. These conditions give all 142 columns in the
audited module. The invariant even denominator space has 30 columns.

This argument DOES NOT allow an arbitrary noninvariant D to be replaced
by an invariant denominator of the same degree. Taking products of its
group translates can increase the degree.

2. DEGREE-SIX GAUGE AND EXACT INCIDENCE AUDIT
------------------------------------------
For constant t, D'=D+tG and N'=N+(tA/6)epsilon satisfy

  N'(G)=AD',
  B_D'(N')=B_D(N)+tG(div(N)+tA),

provided N(G)=AD. Here epsilon is the six-variable Euler field;
div(A epsilon/6)=2A. This is a modulo-G gauge, not a strict B=0 gauge.
It preserves the support on G=0 and coprimality with G.

The independent auditor reconstructs the first matrix with SymPy rather
than importing any upstream polynomial routines. Its rank is 158 on
172 columns. All 10 pure numerator kernel vectors, the three supplied
denominator lifts, and the Euler gauge form a verified basis of its
14-dimensional kernel. Each lies in the complete polynomial module above.
Thus retaining all 10 kernel corrections after quotienting the denominator
space by G loses no possibilities.

The full eligible denominator space is exactly

  span(p E_X0(G), E_X1(G), P E_Y0(G), E_Y1(G)).

It has dimension four and contains G. The quotient by G has dimension
three. Reducing B_D(N) as a polynomial in r is valid: G has constant
leading r-coefficient 21, so remainder zero is exactly membership in (G).
The elementary symmetric variables themselves are algebraically independent.

Independently reconstructed second equations have 161 coefficient rows,
36 quadratic terms, rank 30. Their row space contains each of a0^2,
a1^2,a2^2, with all ten numerator-kernel parameters cancelled. The three
explicit row-combination witnesses in degree6_square_obstruction_witnesses.json
were rechecked coefficient by coefficient. Hence a0=a1=a2=0 over every
characteristic-zero field, including C and any algebraic extension of Q.

CONCLUSION: every invariant, Y-even degree-six normalized certificate
denominator would be a scalar multiple of G, hence illegal. This is an
exact algebraic-closure obstruction, not a finite-prime-field screen.
The evidence and its input hashes are in independent_degree6_audit.json.

3. ALL DENOMINATORS OF DEGREE AT MOST THREE, WITH NO SYMMETRY ASSUMPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Modulo five, dG/dZi = 2 Zi^5. For d<=3 consider the combined linear map

  (N0,...,N5,D) -> sum_i Ni (dG/dZi) - A D,

with deg Ni=d+1 and deg D=d. Modulo five its monomial columns are
2 Zi^5 m and -A n. They occupy distinct monomial rows. Two different
Zi^5 blocks cannot overlap: overlap would have total degree at least ten,
whereas every column has degree d+6<=9. An A n column has each exponent
at most d+1<=4, so it cannot meet any Zi^5 column.

Thus a selected square integer minor is diagonal modulo five, with
entries 2 and -1. Its determinant is nonzero over Q, and stays nonzero
under any characteristic-zero field extension. The map is injective.
The normalized first equation forces N=0 and D=0.

This excludes all nonzero normalized denominators of degrees zero through
three, even with algebraic coefficients and without permutation invariance
or parity restrictions. Passing to an algebraic coefficient field cannot
create a cubic certificate and then rescue it by a field norm.

The Lean theorem SmallDenominatorObstruction.protected_verified_denominator_degree_ge_four
now proves the ordinary-certificate adapter as well. It accepts the actual
Verify F A F.totalDegree q D N Q record with unrestricted Q. Legality gives
F | AD-N(F); projection to homogeneous degree deg(D)+6 supplies a
homogeneous quotient H. Adding H epsilon/6 produces the normalized first
equation without changing D or its degree. Applying the uniform obstruction
contradicts D!=0 unless deg(D)>=4. The endpoint axioms are only propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound. This is a lower bound for any certificate,
not a proof that a certificate exists.

4. U/V PRODUCT GEOMETRY AND AN INDEPENDENT SUPPORT OBSTRUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------
This section uses standard complex algebraic geometry and Hodge theory;
it has not been formalized in Lean.

For U-support put a=5/7,b=10/7, and form

  C' = {U(X)=0, t^2=q(X)} in P^3,
  S_ab = {sum zi^6+a q(z)s^4+b q(z)^2 s^2=0} in P^3.

There is a dominant rational map

  C' x S_ab --> {F=U=0}, ([X:t],[z:s]) |-> [sX:tz].

The substitution identity is checked exactly in norm_support_audit.py.
On the dense locus q(X)!=0 the map is generically finite. For V-support
the same construction uses the Y curve and a=10/7,b=5/7. This swaps the
two coefficients in the auxiliary surface, not in the protected target.

Here is the complete singularity check for S_ab. At s=0 its z-gradient
has coordinates 6zi^5, so no projective singularity occurs there. At s=1,
the s-derivative is 2q(2a+bq), and each z-gradient is

  6zi^5+c(p-zi),  c=a+2bq.

Consequently q is either zero or -2a/b.

When q=0 and the three zi are distinct, the cubic with these roots must
divide 6T^5+c(p-T). Its remainder is

  6(p^3-2pq+r)T^2 + [6(-p^2q+q^2+pr)-c]T
  +6(p^2-q)r+cp.

At q=0, vanishing forces r=-p^3, c=-6p^4, and -12p^5=0,
contradicting c=a!=0. With repeated roots (x,x,y), q=0 implies x=0
or y=-x/2. The first gives only the origin. In the second case the
two gradient equations imply x^4=-c/12 and (129/64)c x=0, impossible.
The origin is an ordinary node: its Hessian has determinant 2a^3!=0.

When q=-2a/b, c=-3a. For three distinct roots the same remainder gives:
if p=0 then b^2+8a=0; if p!=0, setting h=p^2/q and k=b^2/(8a)
gives h^2-h-1=k and -h^2+3h-2=k. Eliminating h yields
b^4+8ab^2-16a^2=0. Neither exceptional value vanishes for either
actual pair (a,b).

For repeated roots (x,x,t x), the equations instead imply

  f(t)=t^6+t^5-2=0,
  b^2(1+t)(1+2t)^2-8a=0.

The latter polynomial is a nonzero scalar multiple of
20t^3+40t^2+25t-9 in the U case, or
20t^3+40t^2+25t-107 in the V case. Exact integer Bezout identities
S f+T g=C, with C respectively 2564052964 and 66604892794, exclude
common roots over C. Their full coefficients are in norm_support_audit.json.
These cases include all-equal roots. Thus S_ab has precisely the one node.

The same q=0 argument shows that the conic q=0 intersects the smooth
Fermat plane sextic U=0 transversely. The branched double cover C' is
therefore smooth and connected. The surface S_ab is irreducible (otherwise
two components meet in a singular curve). Its resolution has h^{1,0}=0:
a hypersurface in P^3 has H^1(O)=0 by the usual hypersurface exact sequence
and projective-space cohomology, and an ordinary surface node is rational.

S3(X) acts only on the C' factor in the U construction. By Kunneth,
H^{2,0}(C' x resolved S_ab) is H^{0,0}(C') tensor H^{2,0}(resolved S_ab),
so this entire space is trivial under S3(X). Resolving the rational map
and using injectivity of pullback under a generically finite dominant map
implies that the resolution of {F=U=0} has no sign(S3(X)) piece in H^{2,0}.
The analogous statement for V uses S3(Y).

The target residue A Omega/F^2 transforms by the sign character under
each S3: A and F are symmetric but the volume form transforms by the
determinant. Its leading (3,1) class is nonzero. An elementary check of
A notin J(F) suffices: any degree-six Jacobian representation uses linear
coefficients. Comparing Zi^5 Zj forces every off-diagonal coefficient zero;
comparing Zi^6 then forces the diagonal coefficients zero. The cross terms
of F have exponents at most two, so linear differentiation cannot produce
any of those monomials. This contradicts A!=0.

For smooth F, Griffiths's residue theorem identifies the resulting nonzero
(3,1) class. The Gysin description of supported cohomology says that a
(3,1) class supported on a divisor comes from H^{2,0} of its resolution.
The missing sign piece therefore excludes U-support and V-support, including
all pole multiplicities. This is an independent geometric obstruction,
with the standard Hodge/resolution results stated as external inputs.

5. WHAT THESE RESULTS DO AND DO NOT LEAVE OPEN
---------------------------------------------
The exact invariant degree-six calculation closes the algebraic-extension
loophole in the previous prime-field grids. The degree-at-most-three result
also closes the suggested noninvariant cubic/field-norm route. Norms and
group products can still increase support degree, and neither argument
excludes arbitrary higher-degree or noninvariant supports.

These statements do not construct a legal denominator, six numerators, a
source-matching trace identity, or a full HasCertificate theorem. They
identify places where a proposed workaround cannot work, rather than fill
the remaining geometric existence-to-explicit-certificate gap.
