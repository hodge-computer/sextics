INDEPENDENT AUDIT: INVARIANT DENOMINATORS OF DEGREE AT MOST EIGHT
==============================================================

Result
------
The exact CAS calculation excludes every nonzero, legal, homogeneous,
S3(X) x S3(Y)-invariant, Y-even certificate denominator of degree at most
eight for the protected symmetric-sextic-residual target over C. This also
excludes such denominators with coefficients in any characteristic-zero
subfield or algebraic extension. This is not a Lean theorem and does not
exclude noninvariant denominators or invariant denominators of larger degree.
No certificate is constructed.

Independent reconstruction
--------------------------
independent_degree8_audit.py imports no polynomial routines from the original
generator. It uses SymPy's sparse polynomial engine, with variables ordered
r,p,q,P,Q,R so lexicographic division reduces by the leading r^2 term of
G=7F. Rational matrix operations use FLINT. It establishes:

  first matrix: 322 rows, 324 numerator and 64 denominator columns, rank 318;
  full first-equation kernel: dimension 70;
  pure numerator kernel: dimension 48;
  denominator gauges G times invariant even degree-two polynomials: dimension 4;
  eligible denominator quotient by these gauges: dimension 18.

Each of the 70 supplied kernel/gauge vectors is verified directly. Its
invariant-coordinate field is reconstructed in the complete polynomial
E0,E1,E2 bases by Cramer's rule with exact zero remainders. Its ambient
divergence is independently checked using the discriminant Jacobian formula.
The complete free-module argument is explained in norm_support_scope.txt.
The 70 vectors are independent, exhaust the first-equation kernel, and have
the correct degrees and parities. No numerator-kernel direction is discarded.

General denominator gauge
-------------------------
When deg D=d>=6 and B is homogeneous of degree d-6, put

  D'=D+BG,  N'=N+(AB/6)epsilon.

Assuming N(G)=AD, Euler's identity gives

  N'(G)=AD',
  B_D'(N')=B_D(N)+G*(B div N-N(B)+AB^2),

where B_D(N)=D div N-N(D). This is a modulo-G gauge. It preserves the
ordinary certificate equations and legality; strictness is not assumed.
The four-dimensional degree-two B-space is retained completely. The audit
also checks this identity for all four gauge generators against all eighteen
eligible denominator lifts, giving 72 exact polynomial checks.

Necessary quadrics
------------------
The auditor reconstructs the raw second-equation matrix independently,
then orders all 864 mixed denominator/kernel columns before the 171 pure
quadratic columns. The resulting 435 by 1035 rational matrix has rank 435;
the mixed columns have rank 391. Direct elimination gives 44 pure rational
quadrics, exactly matching degree8_pure_quadrics.json coefficient by
coefficient. The 96 MB intermediate RREF JSON is not loaded or relied upon.

This elimination is a necessary-condition argument: the mixed symbols
represent ai*kj, and every row combination cancelling all of them vanishes
at every actual certificate solution. Treating them independently cannot
invalidate necessity. Sufficiency of these quadrics is not needed.

Independent second-prime Macaulay computation
--------------------------------------------
independent_degree8_macaulay.py constructs the degree-four multiplication
map using exponent vectors, a permutation of the eighteen variables,
reversed quadrics, multiplier-first columns, and a transpose of the original
matrix orientation. It accumulates coefficients rather than assigning them.

Modulo 103 the resulting matrix has 5985 rows and 7524 columns, with rank
5985. All rational coefficient denominators are units modulo 103. This
independently confirms the original full-rank result modulo 101.

A nonzero maximal minor modulo 103 is nonzero over Q. Consequently the
rational degree-four multiples of the 44 quadrics span the entire space of
degree-four polynomials in eighteen variables. Their ideal contains every
ai^4. At any common zero over C, every ai therefore vanishes. There is no
remaining projective denominator parameter point, including points defined
only over algebraic extensions. This is a rank-and-ideal argument, not an
enumeration of F_103-rational points.

The zero parameter tuple means D belongs to G times the degree-two gauge
space. Such a nonzero denominator is illegal. Thus degree eight is excluded.

Extension to degrees below eight
--------------------------------
Let p=X0+X1+X2. This is invariant and Y-even. It is a prime linear
polynomial, and does not divide F: at X=(1,-1,0), Y=(0,0,0), p=0 but F=2.
Therefore p is relatively prime to F.

For any putative legal invariant even D of degree d<=8, multiply D and N
by H=p^(8-d). The new denominator is nonzero, homogeneous of degree eight,
invariant, Y-even, and still relatively prime to F. Moreover,

  (HN)(F)=A(HD),
  B_(HD)(HN)=H^2 B_D(N).

For the unnormalized certificate identity the cleared defect similarly
multiplies by H^2. Thus either presentation produces a degree-eight
certificate, contradicting the audited degree-eight obstruction.
