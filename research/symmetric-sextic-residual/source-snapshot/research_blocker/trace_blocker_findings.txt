EXACT FINDINGS ON THE REMAINING TRACE BLOCKER
===========================================

No full source trace is evaluated in this report. The findings below are
proved restrictions and algebraic reductions, with their scope stated
explicitly. They should not be read as a proof of the final certificate.

1. Exactly one universal coefficient relation
---------------------------------------------
The script trace_operand_rank.py reconstructs the monic cubic of the exact
protected sextic, its r,s derivatives, and the universal moving-point source.
It imposes

    G(alpha^2,beta)=0,
    G_w Y+2 alpha G_x Z+U G_s-V G_r=0,

clears the already prescribed unit G_w, and reduces modulo the monic point
cubic in beta. At lambda=r=s=1 the nine coefficient rows occupy27 monomial
columns and have exact rational rank8. An explicit nonzero8-by-8 minor and
its column monomials are recorded in trace_operand_rank.json.
The point cubic is squarefree over Q(alpha): its discriminant at alpha=1 is
-305637348492/28561. Thus localization by G_w introduces no hidden loss of
components in this universal coefficient-rank calculation.

The only relation at this point, in source order
(M1[0],...,M1[4],M2[0],...,M2[3]), is

    M2[3] - (5316/2291)*M1[4] = 0.

This is the specialization of the already known generic highest-coefficient
relation C2*M1[4]-C1*M2[3]=0. That generic relation gives rank at most8, while
the exact specialized nonzero minor gives generic rank at least8. Thus there
is exactly one relation over Q(lambda,r,s) obtained from the universal point
and motion equations alone. The prescribed target obeys it.

This excludes the hoped-for additional linear coefficient cancellations
without contact-family information. It does not exclude new relations after
taking the specific contact trace, or differential identities between traces.

2. A concrete obstruction to the simplest Euler-Jacobi shortcut
--------------------------------------------------------------
The44 point-scaled equations consist of30 cubics and14 quadrics over
Q(lambda,r,s). Their HIGHEST HOMOGENEOUS PARTS all vanish on the linear space

    rho0=...=rho4=t=mu1=...=mu4=A1=A5=A6=A7=0.

There are30 free coordinates:

    A2,A3,A4; B0,...,B7; g2_0,...,g2_18.

The leading-form system therefore contains an explicit projective P^29.
The six small-open inverse equations also have highest parts vanishing there
with their inverse coordinates unrestricted, giving a P^35 for that input.
These assertions were checked exactly over Q(lambda,r,s), not only modulo a
prime; see trace_infinity_obstruction.py and its JSON receipt.

The reason is structural. Cubic leading terms involve only rho0,...,rho4,t.
The remaining quadratic equations have odd a,b monomial parity. With the
variable-leading part of Q2 zero and A restricted to its a-parity half,
A^2, B^2 and the leading Q2*g2 term have no such surviving coefficient.

Consequently the elementary residue theorem requiring no common projective
zero of the displayed highest forms does not apply. Polynomial degree alone
cannot be used to declare the desired residues zero.

This is a statement about the naively homogenized INPUT GENERATORS. It is
not a proof of an affine positive-dimensional component, and is not a claim
about the saturated projective closure of the completed ideal. A completed
basis can derive further leading relations that remove this input stratum.
The computation identifies a genuine obstacle to the simple residue shortcut;
it does not by itself prove the precise source of every solver allocation.

The obvious Q2*linear presentation gauge is already absent on Delta. For
the even cubic, the omitted a^2*b and a*b^2 coefficients give the matrix
[[1,mu0],[mu1,1]], with determinant1-mu0*mu1. For the odd cubic, the omitted
a*b*c and a*b*d coefficients give the identity matrix. Thus those four
directions are not a hidden positive-dimensional freedom on the chart.

3. A geometric degree that can be proved without solving the contact curve
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix a smooth invariant quadric S=(Q2=0) over an algebraically closed field of
characteristic zero. Let sigma negate c,d. On this quadric,

    dim H0(S,O(3))=16,
    dim H0(S,O(6))^+=25.

The norm construction is the morphism

    nu:P^15 -> P^24,       [h] |-> [h*sigma(h)].

It has no base point: S is integral, so a nonzero section h has nonzero norm.
It is defined by quadrics, hence nu^*O(1)=O(2). It is finite onto its image:
for any fixed nonzero norm section, the effective divisor of h must be a
subdivisor of its fixed divisor, and there are only finitely many such
subdivisors; a divisor determines its projective section uniquely.

A general (3,3) divisor on the smooth quadric is irreducible and distinct
from its translate. Its norm has exactly those two irreducible components,
so its only projective preimages are [h] and [sigma(h)]. Thus nu has generic
degree2. If Y is its image, comparison of leading Hilbert-polynomial terms
(equivalently, hyperplane intersection degrees) gives

    2*degree(Y)=2^15,       degree(Y)=16,384.

Marking a point P with P!=sigma(P) and requiring h(P)=0 restricts the source
to P^14. A general such h does not also vanish at sigma(P), so the marked
norm map has generic degree1: the point selects the factor. Its image has
degree2^14=16,384 and is the norm-image hyperplane section N(P)=0.

This explains precisely what the h coordinates parameterize: a high-degree
rational norm locus. IT DOES NOT COMPUTE OR BOUND FROM BELOW THE DEGREE OF
THE ACTUAL FINITE INCIDENCE ALGEBRA. The Q1,Q2 variation, the target section,
the marked point, special quadric strata and boundary contributions still
have to be intersected. In particular,16,384 must not be reported as the
number of roots of the current system.

4. The canonical form does not supply the missing normalization
--------------------------------------------------------------
The current form eta_R=tau(R dh) is the normalized adjunction/Abel-Jacobi
pullback of R, up to the fixed overall sign convention. Its nonzero value
and its closedness do not prove that pushing it back gives R. The resulting
cohomological operation is the correspondence composition

    S=Gamma_* Gamma^*.

Even when S is known to be invertible on the relevant Hodge sector, recovering
R requires applying S^(-1), and passing to the displayed rational current
also requires the appropriate exact differential correction. Dividing the
literal trace by the degree of its finite map does not establish that inverse.

This qualification is explicit in the inspected norm-contact proof, section
3B.3 following formula(3B.3.4), and in its supported-projector section3B.6.
There is presently no evaluation proving S(R)=n*R for this chosen source,
nor a computed inverse/correction for it. Accordingly the nine comparisons
remain candidate conditions, not identities guaranteed by the canonical
residue formula. They could fail even after the finite algebra is computed.

5. A sound determinant-free repair of the algebra presentation
-------------------------------------------------------------
The independently reviewed Jacobian-witness theorem is sound. Two independent
generic systems Jv=b, Jw=c retain exactly the full Jacobian open. For fixed
RHSs, a COMPLETED zero-dimensional augmented quotient proves the same thing,
even with one RHS, because any consistent singular root would carry an
unrestricted positive-dimensional kernel fiber. No reduction assumption is
needed. This supplies a rigorous acceptance gate rather than a heuristic
random saturation.

The nineteen g2 equations have an invertible pivot block on Delta. The exact
Schur-complement identities in trace_jacobian_witness_review.txt reduce the
Jacobian witness to25 coordinates per RHS after eliminating that block.
They preserve the entire localized algebra. They can also be used while
retaining the original variables via an adjugate19-by-19 block, avoiding
expansion of the full44-by-44 determinant. The degree increase after block
elimination remains a practical tradeoff.

The emitted25-variable system has now passed an independent exact GENERIC
polynomial audit:44 row bindings,625 gradient entries and1100 off-ideal
chain-rule identities, with lambda,r,s also left symbolic. Its Jacobian is
delta1^17*delta2^20*delta3^24 times the original44-variable Jacobian on the
equation ideal. See trace_schur25_generic_receipt.json. This verifies that
neither reconstruction derivatives nor derivatives of row-clearing factors
were dropped.

6. A sparser polynomial reduction, independently audited
--------------------------------------------------------
The better intermediate reduction writes

    Q2=ab+M,       g2=E+ab*O,       R=R_even+ab*T,

with M,E,O even in the a,b exponents. The odd equations have an identity
pivot block and give E=-M*O-T. The remaining polynomial is

    R_even-M*T+(a^2*b^2-M^2)*O.

This eliminates14 g2 coefficients without denominators or any new open.
Keeping the two linear point equations for mu0,A0 yields32 core variables;
keeping their eliminations yields30. Both preserve cubic degree, avoiding the
degree9 expansion of the full25-variable elimination.

Independent generic polynomial replays checked the sparse30 and sparse32
packets against the original equations. For sparse32 this includes46 source
rows,1024 reduced derivatives,448 reconstruction derivatives,1472 off-ideal
chain rules,44 bindings back to the point-scaled source, both linear point
equations and all six original opens including their inverse equations.
The actual determinant relation is J32=J46=J44_point, with no unit factor.
See trace_sparse30_audit_receipt.json and trace_sparse32_audit_receipt.json.

These are exact presentation and derivative checks, not finite-basis results.
A small-open sparse32 solver result still needs the full Jacobian and selected
residue/regularity localizations before it represents the requested traces.

7. The new quadratic-net bypass is ruled out for the original blocks
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The new upstream quadratic-net theorem requires the sextic to factor through
three quadratic forms in one ternary block. The mixed coefficient of the
actual target forces q=xy+xz+yz into that net, while setting the other block
to zero requires x^6+y^6+z^6 to be a cubic in the same net.

The accompanying proof trace_quadratic_net_obstruction.txt rules this out for
ARBITRARY nets, including ones without S3 symmetry. Restriction to q=0 gives
the degree12 binary form (u^6+v^6)(u+v)^6+u^6v^6. Any proposed net would
partition its twelve roots into three linearly dependent quartic factors.
At the good split prime1459, every one of the5775 partitions has rank3.
Reduction of monic algebraic-integer factors carries any hypothetical
characteristic-zero dependency to one of these partitions, a contradiction.

The pure-stdlib verifier and complete minor certificate are
trace_quadratic_net_obstruction.py and trace_quadratic_net_certificate.json.
The proof applies to either original block and arbitrary within-block linear
coordinate changes. It does not exclude transformations mixing the blocks,
other geometric arguments, or a certificate for the original problem.

What remains open
-----------------
No completed full finite quotient, degree, or individual source trace is
produced here. The new determinant-free presentation can address singular
affine components without an expanded Jacobian determinant, but its
zero-dimensional certificate still has to finish. Even successful evaluation
of the nine traces must be distinguished from a proof that the chosen
canonical form has the needed push-pull inverse and differential correction.
