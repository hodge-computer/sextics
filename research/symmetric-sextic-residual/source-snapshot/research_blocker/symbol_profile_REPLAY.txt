The CLI, reducer choice, native metadata, and checkpoint format are unchanged.
Use the same --input, --resume-state, --output, --checkpoint, --threads,
--pairs, and --round-budget arguments as with the frozen OpenMP binary.
No target computation was run during this variant's construction or validation.

At a safe checkpoint, immediately before serialization, the driver prints one
[symbol_profile] line. It also prints one on successful completion.

Counters and times are PROCESS-LOCAL: they start from zero on each execution,
including native resume. They are not stored in or restored from the sidecar.

lookup_ns / lookup_seconds:
  CLOCK_MONOTONIC time from entry to find_multiplied_reducer until a divisor
  is found or the leading list is exhausted. Includes the original metadata
  setup, mask scan, full exponent comparisons, quotient-exponent preparation,
  and the small profiling overhead within that region.

materialization_ns / materialization_seconds:
  CLOCK_MONOTONIC time around only multiplied_poly_to_matrix_row called from
  find_multiplied_reducer. Includes its allocation, multiplication, and hash
  insertion. Excludes row materialization during initial S-pair selection.

lookup_calls = lookup_hits + lookup_misses.
materialized_rows = lookup_hits.
materialized_terms is the sum of source reducer lengths for these rows.
leading_positions is the total length of scanned leading-monomial prefixes,
including positions skipped by the divisor mask.
full_divisibility_tests counts mask-passing candidates whose exponents are
compared, including candidates that fail those comparisons.

These two measured regions need not sum to all symbolic preprocessing time:
outer-loop allocation/bookkeeping and other calls are outside the regions.
Clock calls and counter increments add diagnostic overhead. This variant makes
no speedup claim. The instrumented reducer loop is serial in the frozen OpenMP
source, so its process-local counters require no atomic updates.

Build:
  python symbol_profile_build.py
The builder creates a new immutable vendor/symbol_profile_<hash> directory and
refuses to overwrite an existing one. All frozen source and binary files remain
untouched. Only symbol.c is changed in the copied upstream source tree; the
driver additionally includes the profile helper and prints at two safe exits.

Validation:
  python symbol_profile_check.py
This consumes the frozen OpenMP build's existing small inputs and reference
outputs. Four complete results (8-, 16-, and 32-bit arithmetic, including block
order and a degree-fall case) are byte-identical. A profiled checkpoint/restart
and consumption of a frozen OpenMP sidecar also finish byte-identically.
The native metadata ABI remains 560 bytes. Runtime was below one second.
