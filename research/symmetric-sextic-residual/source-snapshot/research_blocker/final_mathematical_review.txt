2. Generic two-RHS Jacobian localization

The argument is sound over an arbitrary field, including when the starting
scheme is nonreduced. On the reduced corank-delta stratum, the tangent space
lies in the kernel of the original equation Jacobian, so its dimension is
at most delta. The bad incidence of two RHS vectors in the Jacobian image
has dimension at most 2n-delta<2n. Independent generic RHS parameters avoid
all these singular strata.

The resulting absence of singular geometric points makes det(J) a unit in
the augmented algebra, not merely nonzero on selected reduced components.
The adjugate formulas then give the claimed scheme-level isomorphism with
the full Jacobian localization. In the finite-type setting this is a finite
etale algebra, allowing the zero algebra.

For fixed RHS values, a completed zero-dimensional augmented quotient is
also a valid acceptance gate, provided all solve variables are unrestricted.
Any surviving singular point would have a positive-dimensional affine
kernel fiber. Adding extra normalizations can invalidate the argument.
The theorem correctly limits a modular conclusion to that finite-field
fiber and does not silently establish characteristic-zero trace identities.
The remaining residue/regularity opens are still required.
