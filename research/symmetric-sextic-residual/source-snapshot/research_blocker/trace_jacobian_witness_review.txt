INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE JACOBIAN-WITNESS THEOREM
================================================

The proof in jacobian_witness_theorem.txt is sound as written. The conclusions
hold over an arbitrary field, after taking the indicated independent
transcendental right-hand sides. No reducedness or complete-intersection
assumption on the original equations is needed.

1. Scheme-level points checked
-------------------------------
For n equations in n variables, the reduced rank-(n-delta) stratum has
dimension at most delta: the tangent space of that reduced stratum lies in
the kernel of the ORIGINAL equation Jacobian. Consequently the locus of
(x,b,c) with b,c in the image has dimension at most 2n-delta. Its closure
cannot contain the generic point of the 2n-dimensional RHS space. Passing
from K to its algebraic closure does not spoil the independence of the RHS
parameters, since the extension is algebraic.

No singular geometric point survives in the generic augmented algebra.
This means the ideal obtained by adding det(J) is the unit ideal, not just
that det(J) is nonzero on a preferred component. Thus det(J) is a unit even
if the starting algebra was nonreduced. Adjugates then eliminate all solve
coordinates and give exactly the original Jacobian localization after scalar
extension. The result is etale; in this finite-type setting over a field it
is finite and reduced, with the zero algebra allowed.

For FIXED right-hand sides, the claimed zero-dimensional acceptance gate is
also sound. A singular root at which Jv=b is consistent gives an entire
positive-dimensional affine kernel fiber in the augmented scheme. Such a
fiber is impossible in a zero-dimensional finite-type algebra, including a
nonreduced one. A completed zero-dimensional certificate therefore makes
det(J) a unit and proves the same algebra isomorphism. Every nonsingular
original root is retained, because its linear solve has a unique solution.

It is essential that the solve coordinates remain unrestricted. Additional
normalizations can cut the diagnostic kernel fiber down to finitely many
points and invalidate the argument. The counterexamples in the reviewed
note correctly demonstrate this issue.

2. Exact reduction from 44 witness coordinates to 25
----------------------------------------------------
The nineteen g2 coefficients already form an invertible linear block on the
prescribed Delta open. Write z for those19 variables and y for the other25
core variables after point elimination. Reorder equations into

    L(y,z)=M(y) z-R(y)=0,          E(y,z)=0,

where L has19 entries and E has25. The exact pivot matrix in
../proof_attempt4/contact_target_circuit.json has determinant

    d=(1-mu0*mu1)(1-2*mu0*mu1)^3(1-3*mu0*mu1).

This factorization was independently recomputed directly from its19-by-19
symbolic matrix. All its factors are already inverted by Delta. Thus

    z=phi(y)=M(y)^(-1) R(y)

is a global algebra identity on the chart; this does not choose a component.
Set g(y)=E(y,phi(y)). The original Jacobian, in (z,y) column order, is

              [ M       L_y ]
    J_full =  [ E_z     E_y ].

Differentiate L(y,phi(y))=0. It gives

    phi_y=-M^(-1)L_y,
    J_g=E_y-E_z M^(-1)L_y =: S.

The following is an explicit block identity over the localized algebra:

    [ I19          0 ] J_full [ I19   -M^(-1)L_y ] = [ M   0 ]
    [-E_z M^(-1) I25 ]        [ 0            I25 ]   [ 0   S ].

Both multiplying matrices have determinant1. Hence, up to the fixed row and
column permutation signs used to create this order,

    det(J_full)=d*det(S).

This identifies the full Jacobian open with the reduced25-variable Jacobian
open. It also identifies witness equations directly. A full RHS of the form
(0,b) is equivalent to

    S v_y=b,
    v_z=-M^(-1)L_y v_y.

Therefore only25 free witness coordinates per RHS are required after exact
g2 elimination. Apply the generic or fixed-RHS theorem to the reduced system
itself. Six small opens remain localizations; their inverse variables need
not be included in the25 core tangent coordinates. If represented explicitly,
their Jacobian block in those inverse variables is diagonal with units.

If each implemented reduced polynomial is gtilde_i=u_i(y)*g_i, with u_i a
unit product of allowed denominator factors, then modulo the equation ideal

    d(gtilde)=diag(u_i)*d(g).

The additional terms g_i*du_i vanish in that quotient. Thus clearing or
dividing those unit factors does not change the Jacobian open. One may
apply the witness theorem directly to the polynomial gtilde system. Constant
generic RHSs for gtilde correspond to y-dependent RHSs for g; this is harmless
when the theorem is applied to gtilde, but they should not be described as
constant generic RHSs for the original44-equation Jacobian.

For the actual emitted schur25_system.json the conventions are now fully
audited. Free rows followed by pivot rows have115 inversions; core columns
followed by g2 columns have19 inversions, so the combined sign is positive.
The reduced equations F satisfy H_free_i(y,phi)=(rowfactor_i/D)*F_i, where
D=delta1*delta2*delta3. The product of the25 row factors is delta1^7*delta2^2.
Therefore the exact Jacobian conversion on the equation ideal is

    J25=delta1^17*delta2^20*delta3^24*J44.

The independent trace_schur25_audit.py --generic replay verified all44 source
row identities, all625 emitted reduced derivatives, and all1100 off-ideal
chain-rule identities over the polynomial ring in the25 core variables and
lambda,r,s, with the sole lambda-only inverse treated as an independent
scalar. In particular it checked the nonvanishing off-ideal correction:

    D^2*S_ik = D*rowfactor_i*J25_ik
               +(D*d_k(rowfactor_i)-rowfactor_i*d_k(D))*F_i.

It did not freeze reconstructed g2 coefficients. The receipt is
trace_schur25_generic_receipt.json. This is an exact generic equation and
gradient audit; it does not certify a fixed RHS, a quotient basis, or a trace.

3. A version retaining the original variables
---------------------------------------------
If retaining z avoids expensive rational substitutions, set T=adj(M) and

    Shat=d*E_y-E_z*T*L_y.

The purely polynomial block identity

    [ I19        0 ] J_full = [ M       L_y  ]
    [-E_z*T  d*I25]          [ 0       Shat ]

holds exactly. On d!=0, solving Shat*v_y=d*b gives the same witness as above;
v_z=-T*L_y*v_y/d is recovered uniquely. This keeps only25 new witness
coordinates while retaining all44 original coordinates. It avoids expanding
the44-by-44 determinant. Its coefficient degrees can be higher than those of
the original cubic Jv=b system, so the smaller variable count is not by
itself a performance guarantee.

4. Current limit
----------------
These identities give an exact, potentially cheaper presentation of the
Jacobian open. They do not establish that a displayed fixed RHS is good until
the augmented quotient has been completely verified to be zero-dimensional.
They do not evaluate the remaining residue-matrix and regularity opens, or
any of the nine source traces. A modular completed quotient concerns that
fiber unless an additional characteristic-zero specialization argument is
supplied.
