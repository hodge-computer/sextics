NO TERNARY QUADRATIC NET CONTAINING q REPRESENTS THE FERMAT SEXTIC
================================================================

Theorem
-------
Let q=xy+xz+yz and f=x^6+y^6+z^6. There is no complex vector space W of
ternary quadratic forms, of dimension at most3, such that q belongs to W
and f is a homogeneous cubic polynomial in elements of W.

This is a general obstruction, not a search restricted to symmetric nets.
The proof uses the accompanying exhaustive finite certificate. It does not
assert that the original sextic certificate is impossible.

1. Restrict to the fixed smooth conic
-------------------------------------
The map

    [u:v] -> [u(u+v):v(u+v):-uv]

parametrizes q=0. Its three coordinates never vanish simultaneously, and
substitution verifies q=0. Restricting a ternary quadratic to this conic
gives a binary quartic. The restricted sextic is the binary degree12 form

    F12(u,v)=(u^6+v^6)(u+v)^6+u^6v^6.

In the v=1 chart its monic polynomial is

    F12(t,1)=t^12+6t^11+15t^10+20t^9+15t^8+6t^7+3t^6
              +6t^5+15t^4+20t^3+15t^2+6t+1.

Its value at [u:v]=[1:0] is1, so no root is at infinity.

If W has the asserted property, choose its basis beginning with q and
complete it to q,Q1,Q2 if necessary. On q=0, the restricted f is a binary
cubic in the two quartics A=Q1|q and B=Q2|q. Over C that binary cubic factors
into three linear forms. Consequently F12 is a product of three quartics
lying in the two-dimensional space spanned by A and B.

The good-prime calculation below also proves F12 is squarefree in
characteristic zero. Therefore each quartic factor has four distinct roots,
their three root sets are disjoint, and the three quartic coefficient vectors
are linearly dependent. None has a root at infinity, so all may be normalized
to monic univariate quartics. It is enough to exclude EVERY partition of the
twelve roots into three groups of four with linearly dependent monic quartics.

2. The exact good-prime certificate
-----------------------------------
The prime1459 gives the following twelve DISTINCT roots of F12(t,1):

    41,188,426,550,633,660,798,825,908,1032,1270,1417.

The verifier checks primality by trial division and multiplies all twelve
linear factors, recovering every coefficient of F12 modulo1459. Thus this
is a complete squarefree factorization, not a partial root list.

There are

    12!/(4!^3*3!) = choose(11,3)*choose(7,3) = 5775

unordered partitions. The verifier enumerates them without repetition: the
first group contains root index0; the second contains the least remaining
index; the third is the complement.

For each partition let a,b,c be the coefficient vectors of its three monic
quartics. Since their leading coefficients are all1, their rank is less
than3 exactly when the two four-coordinate vectors a-c and b-c are
proportional. For EVERY one of the5775 partitions the verifier supplies a
nonzero2-by-2 minor of those difference vectors. Thus every triple has
rank3 over F1459.

The complete list of nonzero minor witnesses is stored in
trace_quadratic_net_certificate.json. The pure-stdlib program
trace_quadratic_net_obstruction.py verifies the integer conic substitution,
the complete modular factorization, the partition count and all5775 minors.

3. Why this proves nonexistence over C
--------------------------------------
Let L be a number field containing all roots of the monic integer polynomial
F12. Its roots are algebraic integers. A monic quartic whose roots are a
four-element subset therefore also has algebraic-integer coefficients.

If a dependent triple existed over C, its monic factors would be precisely
three such root-subset polynomials. Their3-by-3 coefficient minors would
vanish as algebraic integers. Reduce at any prime ideal of L above1459.
Since the polynomial splits into12 distinct roots modulo1459, the reductions
of the12 characteristic-zero roots remain distinct and exhaust the displayed
Fp roots. The three root subsets reduce to one of the5775 partitions.

Every zero algebraic-integer minor reduces to zero. This would give a
dependent quartic triple over F1459, contradicting the exhaustive nonzero
minor certificate. Hence no dependent triple exists over C, and the claimed
quadratic net cannot exist. No numerical approximation, heuristic modular
lifting, or degree assumption about a Groebner basis is used.

4. Consequence for the actual symmetric sextic
----------------------------------------------
Write qX=X0X1+X0X2+X1X2 and qY=Y0Y1+Y0Y2+Y1Y2. The protected target is

    F=sum Xi^6 + sum Yi^6 + (5/7)qX^2*qY + (10/7)qX*qY^2.

Suppose F=G(X,Q0(Y),Q1(Y),Q2(Y)), with G of weighted degree6 and the Q's
quadratic. Its coefficient of X0^2*X1^2 is (5/7)qY. In a weighted degree6
expression, that coefficient must be linear in the Q's. Thus qY belongs to
their net. Setting X=0 also expresses the Fermat sextic sum Yi^6 as a cubic
in that net. The theorem above is a contradiction.

After interchanging X and Y, the coefficient of Y0^2*Y1^2 is (10/7)qX, giving
the same contradiction. The conclusion allows arbitrary nets and arbitrary
linear changes within either block, because pulling such a net back gives
a net in the original variables. It does not cover a coordinate change that
mixes the two blocks or a different support construction.

This rules out direct application through either original block of the
quadratic-net theorem in the new upstream source:

https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/blob/a2716a5d55e176ae5f999483b4b3115831385216/docs/03c-quadratic-nets.md

Reproduction
------------
    python trace_quadratic_net_obstruction.py

Only the Python standard library is required. A successful receipt proves
this specific nonexistence theorem. It is not a proof of the original target,
and it is not a proof that the original target is unsolvable.
