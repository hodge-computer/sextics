EXACT TRACE COMPUTATION WITH LOWER MEMORY: INDEPENDENT AUDIT
==========================================================

Scope and status
----------------
This note audits the point-scaled equations, the transport of the ACTUAL nine
source operands, and the eight B-pivot quotients. It gives a concrete route to
their traces without constructing nine dense multiplication matrices. It does
not contain an evaluated full trace, a completed coordinate algebra, or a full
Lean certificate. The nine source comparisons remain candidate conditions:
the chosen canonical contact form has not been proved to have the required
push-pull normalization.

Files inspected: point_scaled_system.py, point_scaled_current.py, and
sign_quotient_current.py in this directory. The independent B-pivot equation
audit in point_scaled_Bpivot_systems.json confirms the coordinate-order sign
used below. Modular controls in the current-transport receipts are evaluations
at points satisfying the two eliminated point equations. They are explicitly
NOT contact solutions or trace computations.

1. The first quotient preserves the complete requested algebra
-------------------------------------------------------------
For a coefficient multiplying a monomial with total c,d degree k, put

    hat(c) = alpha^k c,          t = alpha^2.

The moving point becomes (a,b,c,d)=(1,r,1,s). The eliminated coefficients are
hat(mu0) and hat(A0); each has coefficient 1 in its point equation, so no new
localization is introduced by this elimination. There are 44 remaining
variables and 44 equations of total degree at most 3.

This is the quotient by T_B:(B,alpha)->(-B,-alpha), not the quotient by alpha
sign alone. On the prescribed alpha open it is an etale quadratic cover:
adjoin alpha with alpha^2=t. The residual involution is simultaneous sign
reversal of all hat(A) and hat(B). The original nine source operands are
invariant under both involutions.

The full open remains essential. In the matrix representation it is

    t * Delta * beta * G_w * det(Jbar) * det(Tbar) * det(Dbar10) != 0.

The selected residue and regularity minors cannot simply be omitted from a
completed trace. They can remain unexpanded matrix frames while the algebra
is computed, provided their unit localizations are subsequently imposed
exactly. The small-open solver inputs are not yet the full requested algebra.

The exact weight totals are 78 for contact coefficients and 132 for equation
rows. The incidence variable change has determinant 2*alpha^79. The two
linear-pivot column moves have 74 inversions. Therefore, ON THE INCIDENCE,

    J_old = 2 alpha^(-53) J44,

where J44 is the ordered Jacobian of the 44 reduced equations. The selected
minor scalings are delta9_hat=alpha^399 delta9_old and
D10_hat=alpha^534 D10_old. All multiplying factors are units.

2. Audit of the actual source transport
--------------------------------------
The implementation differentiates the ORIGINAL equations in their ORIGINAL
variables before substituting the new coordinates. This is necessary.

The original 46-by-46 incidence Jacobian has nine odd columns under T_B: the
eight B columns and the alpha column. Factoring alpha from them gives

    J_old = alpha^9 det(Jbar).

For the cofactor kernel of its first 44 rows, an ordinary contact column has
factor alpha^8, while a B column has factor alpha^7. The degree-nine residue
matrix factors as D_row Tbar D_col, with row and column parity exponents 0 or
1. When both exponents are 1 the corresponding Tbar entry requires an extra
t^(-1); the code includes it. Its normalization row has right-hand side 96.

Writing Tbar*sigma=96*e_last, the 16 contact-form components have the factors
eta_A=alpha^(-1)L_A and eta_B=L_B. Consequently

    f = alpha^7 Fbar,
    M_i = Fbar P_i / (t det(Jbar)).

This is the correct transported original source, including its density.
The degree-ten regularity matrix is factored with the same parity rule.
No material source-factor or binding error was found in this implementation.

Do not replace this by a newly defined canonical form in the new coordinates.
For example, if eta is the original closed relative contact form and
hat_eta=eta/alpha, the ordinary form formula is

    hat_eta = t*tauhat(R_t d(hat(h)))
              - (1/2)*tauhat(R_t E_Y(hat(h))) dt,
    R_t = 2bd(a^2-u)(t c^2-lambda^2 u)/ell^2.

Even hat_eta itself satisfies the twisted equation

    d(hat_eta) = -(dt/(2t)) wedge hat_eta

relative to fixed lambda. Dropping its dt term or silently treating it as an
ordinary closed form changes the problem. Transporting the original scalar
operands, as the implementation does, avoids this mistake.

3. A globally valid source solve; no coordinate-pivot branch loss
----------------------------------------------------------------
Over a product finite etale algebra, a nonzero entry need not be a unit. Thus
choosing one nonzero kernel coordinate at a sample point is insufficient for
evaluation in the entire algebra.

Let Hbar be the first 44 rows and first 45 columns of Jbar. Let qbar and hbar
be its last two rows in the 45 contact columns, and qa,ha their last-column
entries. Use the 45-by-45 matrix

    B = [ Hbar ; ha*qbar - qa*hbar ].

Its determinant is det(Jbar), with the exported row/column conventions.
It is therefore a unit on the full open, on every component. Solve

    B v = e_last.

Then Hbar*v=0 and (qbar*v)ha-qa(hbar*v)=1. Each operand is simply

    M_i = (sum_{j=0}^{15} v_(10+j) L_j) P_i / t.

This needs one 45-by-45 solve and one 220-by-220 residue solve, shared by all
nine operands. It uses no expanded cofactor determinant, no arbitrary
component pivot, and no division by qa. The delivered point evaluator now
uses this augmented solve.

4. The second quotient and all eight strata
-------------------------------------------
The full Jacobian open implies that the B-vector is not zero. Indeed, if all
eight B coefficients vanish, the corresponding eight contact columns of
the 44-row contact Jacobian vanish. Its rank is then at most 37, whereas the
full incidence Jacobian requires rank 44 in those rows.

Partition the entire finite algebra into the eight disjoint strata

    B0=...=B(j-1)=0, Bj!=0,          0 <= j <= 7.

On stratum j put b=hat(Bj), z=b^2, hat(Ai)=b*Ai, hat(Bi)=b*Bi, with Bj=1
and the earlier Bi=0. The residual common-sign involution is exactly b->-b.
It is a free quadratic quotient on z!=0. The equations stay cubic, because
their cubic-coefficient dependence is the quadratic expression
z*(A_normalized^2-B_normalized^2). The first stratum still has 44 variables;
stratum j has 44-j variables. Halving the degree of the finite algebra is a
guaranteed benefit. A reduction of the main chart's variable count is not.

For the actual matrix source, the h row has common-sign parity 1 and the
16 A/B columns have parity 1. Thus

    det(Jbar) = b^17 det(J2),
    Kbar_cubic = b^15 K2_cubic.

The residue matrix has only row factors; its h-product rows and its
normalization row are odd. Hence sigma=b^(-1)sigma2 and L=b^(-1)L2.
Also P_i=b*P2_i. The final factors are therefore

    Fbar=b^14 F2,
    M_i=F2 P2_i/(t z det(J2)).

These factors agree with sign_quotient_current.py. Its degree-ten matrix
factors the h columns, whose indices are 165 through 284. The new full open
is t*z*Delta*beta*G_w*det(J2)*det(T2)*det(D2)!=0.

If the eight quotient algebras have ranks n_j and traces tau_ij, then

    rank(original) = 4 sum_j n_j,
    Tr_original(M_i) = 4 sum_j tau_ij,
    normalized Tr_original(M_i) = (sum_j tau_ij)/(sum_j n_j).

All eight strata must be included. Testing just B0!=0 changes the trace unless
the other strata are proved empty. Failure to find sample controls on a
stratum does not prove emptiness. A plain average of normalized stratum traces
is also wrong unless their ranks happen to be equal.

5. Concrete residue simplification: cancel the incidence determinant
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Combining the audited Jacobian relations gives, on the first quotient,

    det(Jbar) = 2 t^(-31) J44,
    M_i = (t^30/2) Fbar P_i / J44.

For the finite etale algebra of the 44 equations on its full open, define the
localized global residue by summing N(x)/J44(x) over all retained geometric
points. Thus its unnormalized trace is exactly

    Tr(M_i) = Res_full_open( (t^30/2) Fbar P_i ; H1,...,H44 ).

This removes the incidence determinant from the residue numerator. It does
not evaluate the residue, and Fbar still requires the residue-matrix solve.

There is a similarly precise B0-chart formula. The exported variable order
has B0 at zero-based position 16. Removing it and appending z makes 27
inversions. The coordinate Jacobian from normalized variables to hats is
-b^13/2: fourteen other cubic coordinates contribute b, and B0 contributes
1/(2b). Consequently

    det(J2) = -4 t^(-31) z^(-15) J44_B0,
    M_i = -(t^30 z^14/4) F2 P2_i / J44_B0.

The B0 trace is therefore the corresponding localized global residue of
-t^30*z^14*F2*P2_i/4. This sign was independently checked against the actual
variable order. The smaller exceptional strata do not acquire this formula
automatically: they need their own coordinate presentation or residue
description. Retaining their trace contribution is still required.

6. A practical low-memory trace pipeline
---------------------------------------
The current bottleneck is obtaining and certifying the COMPLETE finite
coordinate algebra. Nine dense multiplication matrices are unnecessary once
that algebra is available. None of the following removes the need to account
for every component of the full open.

(a) Preferred endpoint: a verified univariate representation.
    Suppose a finite quotient is K[zeta]/(F), with F monic and squarefree, and
    all needed coordinates are represented modulo F. Check completeness,
    not just that the represented points solve H: a representation of a
    proper subset would produce the wrong trace. For instance, identities
    F(L)=0 and x_i=C_i(L) in the localized input ideal, plus the inverse
    substitution checks, certify the two inverse algebra maps.

    If h=N/D and gcd(D,F)=1, compute V=N*D^(-1) mod F. Then

        Tr(h) = coefficient of zeta^(n-1) in (F' V mod F), n=degree(F).

    Proof: interpolation at the distinct roots says the top coefficient of
    any R of degree<n is sum R(x)/F'(x); take R=F'V mod F. This formula uses
    polynomial vectors, not an n-by-n multiplication matrix. Over a finite
    field it works equally for irreducible factors and nonsplit roots.

    Factor F and process factors sequentially if that lowers peak memory.
    Perform the shared 45-by-45 and 220-by-220 source solves in each factor
    field. Add their field traces and degrees. Discard a factor only after
    checking an original open is zero on it. A factor on which an open
    determinant is singular must be filtered exactly, not replaced by a
    successful sample point. One need not expand those determinants in the
    original 44-variable ring.

(b) If there is a certified monomial basis and normal-form oracle instead:

        Tr(h) = sum_i coefficient_of_bi( NF(h*b_i) ).

    Stream one column at a time. This requires O(n) storage for an individual
    output column, not nine dense n-by-n matrices. It trades more reduction
    work for memory. The basis and its exact reduction oracle may still be
    the dominant memory cost.

(c) Residue-dual alternative.
    A certified residue functional on the finite algebra evaluates the nine
    simplified numerators from section 5 directly. One dual vector can be
    enough; no nine multiplication matrices are needed. But the existing
    220-by-220 degree-nine residue solve is for the separate length-96
    ambient complete intersection. It is NOT the residue functional of the
    large incidence algebra. Its existence or nonzero source value cannot
    substitute for the missing incidence functional.

    Nor is an arbitrary dual vector annihilating the equations and satisfying
    one Jacobian normalization enough in an n-dimensional affine algebra:
    those conditions do not in general uniquely select its canonical global
    residue. A verified quotient/duality computation or a justified residue
    construction is required.

(d) Sparse black-box linear algebra is useful only after specifying its
    operator. A multiplication oracle on a known finite basis, or a sparse
    Macaulay operator with a proved sufficient degree/rank and an exact
    completeness certificate, can be processed without storing a dense
    matrix. The original equations alone are not such an oracle.

    A projected Krylov minimal polynomial can miss components. Its degree
    and cyclicity, or all invariant-factor contributions, must be certified.
    Knowing the characteristic polynomial of one linear form alone also
    does not determine the nine arbitrary source traces: one needs their
    polynomial representations in that form, or an equivalent dual
    functional. This is why a sparse-Wiedemann label does not by itself
    solve the current memory bottleneck.

7. What this lets the next computation do
----------------------------------------
Use the cubic quotient presentation and keep the large opens as matrix
frames. Seek a complete finite quotient or univariate representation using
bounded-memory algebra. Certify its completeness and all full-open filtering.
Then reuse the shared source solves and compute only the required trace
functionals, preferably factor by factor, without ever materializing nine
dense multiplication matrices. The degree-four symmetry quotient is exact
only when all eight strata are retained.

There is already one unconditional linear dependence between the two highest
source coefficients, recorded in trace_top_moment.json in the preceding trace
packet. It can remove one redundant comparison after localization by its
nonzero coefficient. It does not evaluate the shared top moment and does not
justify setting either coefficient to zero.

A completed finite-field calculation would initially be a specialized trace,
not a rational-function identity over Q(lambda,r,s). Generic reconstruction
needs degree/height bounds and verification, or direct exact identities. A
nonzero specialized discrepancy refutes a generic comparison only when the
specialization of the full algebra and operand is justified (in particular,
the relevant finite-rank/open data remain valid). In finite characteristic,
compare Tr(M_i)-n*T_i instead of dividing by n: the rank n could be divisible
by the chosen prime even when the algebra is etale.

No full trace has been evaluated by this audit. The exact coordinate and
source changes improve the representation and avoid unnecessary determinant
and multiplication-matrix expansion; they do not establish the nine candidate
source identities or the final primitive.
