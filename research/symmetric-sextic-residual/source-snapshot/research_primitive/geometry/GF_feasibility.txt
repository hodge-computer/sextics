7 November 2026. Read-only foundation audit and mathematical proof assessment.

RESULT

GF is still unproved, and this directory does not supply a rational primitive.
There is a substantial improvement over the earlier library assessment:
openai/math contains actual proofs of Hodge decomposition, Poincare duality,
supported singular cycle constructions, and projective algebraic de Rham
comparison. These are not present in the target's pinned mathlib alone.
However, their current callable endpoints do not supply the contact-residue
comparison or the algebraic support-to-function-field implication required
here. No GF axiom, primitive hypothesis, or assumed exactness wrapper was added.

THE PRECISE REMAINING GOAL

Over a characteristic-zero field K, let the actual smooth weighted sextic be

  T = {G(a,b,c,d,u)=0} in P(1,1,1,1,2),
  sigma(a,b,c,d,u) = (a,b,-c,-d,u).

Let Lambda be the completed contact-parameter curve and Gamma its actual
incidence cycle in Lambda x T. The finite jet calculation is a calculation
of the normal-obstruction residue functional at a selected point of Lambda.
The missing GF conclusion is

  restriction : H^3_dR(T/K)^- -> H^3(Omega^bullet_{K(T)/K})
  restriction = 0.

For the protected source this must yield a rational 2-form beta satisfying

  d_rel beta = ((G_u R_u - G_uu R)/G_u^3) db wedge dc wedge dd,
  R = 2 b d (a^2-u)(c^2-lambda^2 u) / ell(lambda)^2,
  ell(lambda) = (14 lambda^6 - 10 lambda^4 - 5 lambda^2 + 14)/7,

with one consistently fixed residue-orientation convention. The existing
polynomial chart binding does not by itself construct this differential form.

ONE CHAIN, AND THE EXACT BREAKS

Step A. Construct the actual projective schemes T, Lambda, and the cycle Gamma,
including the etale generic-lambda spreading of the certified contact point.
The external library's SmoothProjectivePresentation is an actual Scheme,
structure morphism, closed immersion into standard Proj, and native Smooth
proof. Its IntegralProjectiveVariety adds only native IsIntegral. Neither
structure assumes any Hodge or comparison theorem. Nevertheless, our current
polynomial/contact data have not yet been turned into these scheme objects.

The weight-two coordinate can be handled by the explicit finite cubic cover
of P^3 with algebra O + O(-2) + O(-4), or by a Veronese projective embedding.
This is a possible implementation route, not a completed construction here.

Step B. Identify the computed residue functional with the actual correspondence.
This is the earliest missing substantive geometric theorem. Its mathematical
signature must include both statements, with the maps defined independently:

  Phi : (S_6 / J(G)_6)^- ~= H^{2,1}(T)^-,
  Phi([R]) = [Res(R Omega_weighted/G^2)],

  (Gamma^*(Phi([R])))|_formal_neighborhood =
       c(t) * tau_t(R_bar * D(h)) dt,                 (AJ)
  c(0) != 0.

Here C_t is the actual genus-four contact curve, h is its cubic equation on
the quadric, D is the actual contact tangent, and tau_t is the Artinian
complete-intersection residue functional normalized by tau(Jac)=96. The
unit c(t) permits a genuine change of residue frame; it may not be chosen
separately for each R. The existing annihilator identities show that the
computed rows kill the indicated polynomial relations. They do not prove
either Phi or (AJ), because they do not identify those quotient modules with
the cohomology of the actual variety/correspondence.

No Abel-Jacobi, coniveau, weighted Griffiths/Jacobian residue, or this contact
normal-sequence comparison theorem was found in the pinned external sources.
The exact searches and inspected endpoint files are recorded in this audit's
provenance. This is a report of the examined development, not a theorem that
no alternative proof exists anywhere.

Step C. Once (AJ) is proved, the jet minor makes Gamma^* injective on H^{2,1}.
The external Hodge decomposition/conjugation, rational correspondence action,
and native Poincare duality are suitable genuine foundations for extending
this to the whole negative H^3 and dualizing to

  Gamma_* : H^1(Lambda)(-1) -> H^3(T)^-   surjective.

The actual correspondence action still must be assembled from the incidence
cycle class and the projections, with its Hodge bidegree and transposition
compatibilities proved for that action. A theorem about a formal linear map
called Gamma does not discharge this step. The previously proved finite
linear algebra can consume these real maps after their construction.

Step D. On the Betti/singular side, support is genuine and available. For
example, the external theorem `relativeGenerator_restrict_away` proves that
the supported cycle generator restricts to zero on an open disjoint from its
actual algebraic support. The supported Thom/Gysin constructions use actual
relative singular cohomology and its restriction maps. They do not merely
attach a 'supported' Boolean or an unexplained support predicate to a class.

After cupping with the pulled-back H^1 class and pushing through the product
projection, one still needs the corresponding support statement for Gamma_*.
The underlying supported singular machinery is present; this specialized
assembly has not been constructed in this checkout.

Step E. The remaining decisive bridge is algebraic support-to-generic exactness.
There are two valid routes; neither was found already completed for this case:

  E1. For every smooth affine open U disjoint from the swept divisor, prove
      the actual map H^3_dR(U/C) -> H^3_B(U(C),C) is injective, and prove its
      naturality with restriction from T. Betti vanishing then implies
      algebraic vanishing on U.

  E2. Construct an algebraic de Rham Gysin/correspondence map with support,
      prove compatibility with the actual Betti map, and prove directly
      that its image restricts to zero off the algebraic support.

The verified-by-source external isomorphism endpoint is PROJECTIVE. Applying
it to T alone does not prove injectivity of the comparison map on U. The
module named Algebraic.ComplexSupport concerns nonnegative COCHAIN DEGREES,
not support on a divisor. The available supported Gysin endpoints examined
here concern singular cohomology, not algebraic de Rham cohomology.

Step F. After algebraic restriction is zero, the external finite-affine
Cech/de Rham computation is available. It computes the actual algebraic
hypercohomology by a Cech differential-form complex. Specializing to an affine
open and comparing the one-open augmentation reduces zero cohomology class
to an actual exact form; localization then produces beta in K(T). This last
passage is elementary homological algebra relative to the preceding work.
Finite field extension and normalized trace can then descend the primitive.

WHY THE FINITE JETS DO NOT ALREADY CONSTRUCT BETA

Inverting the 48 by 48 jet matrix produces linear combinations of local
functionals. It does not by itself produce a global one-form on the completed
parameter curve, a cohomological correspondence map, or a primitive on T.
The formal thickening K[t]/(t^48) is not a replacement for Lambda: differentiation
does not descend to that quotient in characteristic zero and its normalized
finite trace kills positive jets. Serre-dual local principal parts naturally
give the opposite (1,2) piece. Returning to the required (2,1) source invokes
the actual Hodge/conjugation or an independently computed algebraic push-pull
operator. No such step can be silently supplied by the jet rank alone.

MINIMUM GENUINELY NEW PROOF

Even if the extracted external closure compiles, at least the following
mathematical developments remain before GF can be instantiated:

  (i) actual scheme/family/cycle construction and its generic-lambda spread;
  (ii) the specific weighted hypersurface residue isomorphism and (AJ);
  (iii) E1 or E2, with restriction compatibility;
  (iv) binding the resulting rational differential form to the already proved
       normal-current / legal-clearing endpoint for the exact F and A.

This is a real route to formalization, substantially shorter than rebuilding
all Hodge theory in the pinned mathlib. It is not a missing tactic invocation
or an immediately callable theorem establishing RegularGameExists. No time
or line-count estimate is supported by the present source audit. The first
target-specific proof to implement would be the Cech normal-sequence identity
(AJ), using the explicit contact equation and existing residue-annihilator
identities; building all unrelated external theorems first would not solve it.
