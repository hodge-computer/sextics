DIRECT CONTACT-TO-PRIMITIVE INVESTIGATION
7 November 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE
===============
No coefficients b_i of the required rational two-form have been obtained.
No new HasCertificate, RegularGameExists, or primitive-existence theorem is
asserted. In particular, the finite-contact field and the 48-jet matrix do
not by themselves solve the source trace equations.

Using the paper's identification of the canonical residue forms with
global holomorphic pullbacks, there is a useful finite construction from
the actual jet certificate: 48 explicit principal-part classes on the
contact parameter curve, dual to those forms. There is also a concrete obstruction
to treating these local classes as global rational trace weights. The
certified point has no nonconstant function with its sole pole there of
order at most 48. This follows from the same invertible jet matrix and the
global residue theorem; it is not a numerical inference. The holomorphic
extension and curve duality used here remain geometric, unformalized inputs.

The final existence gap can be formulated on the parameter curve without
computing the large moving-point quotient basis, but it still requires a
genuine algebraic de Rham correspondence calculation. The present note
does not replace that calculation by an assumption.

1. EXACT ACTUAL-TARGET INPUTS
===========================
Use the contact component and point P specified by the old contact ball
and the refined dyadic witness in proof_attempt9/contact. The local
parameter is

    t = 4096*(rho0 - 1/4).

The contact coefficient field at P is a finite extension of Q(i): the
44 polynomial equations with rho0 fixed have an invertible 44 by 44
Jacobian. The chosen isolated root is selected by the exact isolating ball.

For the actual 48 selected negative weighted source classes R_i, write

    eta_i = tau(R_i dh) = f_i(t) dt,
    W[n,i] = coefficient of t^n in f_i(t),  0 <= n,i < 48.

The existing witness proves invertibility for a normalization of this
matrix. The canonical row is a common formal-unit multiple of that row,
so W is also invertible. This unit assertion uses the differentiated norm
identity, the complete negative ideal of rank 39, and the old canonical
residue nonvanishing certificate. No zero-containment interval is used as
an exact relation.

All entries of W are algebraic functions of the chosen contact point.
They can be specified by a finite arithmetic circuit: differentiate the
contact equations through order 47, solve each coefficient using the
constant 44 by 44 contact Jacobian, and evaluate the normalized socle
residue through the same order. No moving-point quotient basis is needed
to define W or its inverse. The exact rational replay certifies its
invertibility but is not itself a Lean proof.

2. THE ACTUAL PRINCIPAL-PART DUALS
================================
With rows of W indexed by Taylor order, set

    phi_j(t) = sum_{n=0}^{47} (W^(-1))[j,n] t^(-n-1).

Then, by direct coefficient multiplication,

    Res_P(phi_j eta_i) = delta(j,i).

This is an explicit finite algebraic specification of the 48 principal
parts, using only the actual contact point, the 48 jets, and one matrix
inverse already known to exist. With the Cech convention that pairs a
principal part phi with a holomorphic differential eta by Res_P(phi eta),
their connecting classes in H^1(Lambda,O_Lambda) are independent and dual
to the specified 48-dimensional subspace of holomorphic forms.

The principal parts are not asserted to be global functions with only the
pole P. In fact that assertion is impossible. If f were such a function
with pole order at most 48, write its principal part as sum a_n t^(-n-1).
For every eta_i, the differential f eta_i has no pole outside P. The global
residue theorem gives sum_n a_n W[n,i]=0 for all i. Invertibility of W
forces all a_n=0; f is then a global regular function on the proper
connected curve and is constant. Thus

    H^0(Lambda,O_Lambda(48P)) = the constant field.

Consequently any rational function whose local principal part is a
nonzero phi_j must acquire another pole. Residue contributions from those
other poles cannot be discarded in a trace or source calculation.

3. FINITE SECOND-KIND LIFTS, AND WHAT THEY DO NOT GIVE
===================================================
The principal part d(phi_j) has order at most 49 and has residue zero.
The residue-zero principal-part theorem for a smooth proper curve gives
a meromorphic differential omega_j, regular away from P, with precisely
this principal part. Equivalently, one solves the finite linear map

    H^0(Lambda,Omega(49P)) --> polar differentials at P of order <=49.

Its image is the 48-dimensional residue-zero subspace. Riemann-Roch gives
dim H^0(Omega(49P))=g+48. Here g is the genus of the PARAMETER curve,
at least 48, not the genus four of a contact curve in the threefold.

On U=Lambda minus P, take omega_j; on a sufficiently small affine V about
P, take omega_j-d(phi_j). Together with transition function phi_j these
give a de Rham Cech cocycle. This constructs a lift of the principal-part
class to H^1_dR(Lambda). Different choices differ by a holomorphic form.

The lifting problem concerns ordinary finite linear algebra after a
projective normalization of the actual parameter curve is available.
This can avoid the old 46-variable moving-point trace basis. It does not
make the current coefficients available automatically: one must still
compute the universal correspondence on these de Rham classes, obtain
the desired source in its image, and extract the Cech-to-rational-form
homotopy on the threefold. The 48 principal parts alone pair with the
given H^{2,1} classes; they do not assert the required H^{2,1} source is a
Gysin image without the additional duality/Hodge argument.

4. WHY A WRONSKIAN DIFFERENTIAL-OPERATOR INVERSE IS NOT THE PRIMITIVE
=================================================================
An inverse Wronskian can produce coefficients a_n(t) with

    sum_n a_n D^n f_i = delta(i,j),   D=d/dt.

For every n, repeated integration by parts is the exact identity

    a_n D^n f - f (-D)^n a_n
      = D(sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (-1)^k D^k(a_n) D^(n-1-k)(f)).

Therefore setting a=sum_n(-D)^n(a_n) gives

    f_i a dt = d(delta(i,j)*t - B_i)

for an explicit rational B_i. The right side is exact because t is a
rational function on the parameter curve. This local inverse constructs
zero de Rham classes; it does not create a cohomological right inverse.

5. FINITE TRACE DUALITY DOES NOT ENFORCE CLOSEDNESS
================================================
Let k have characteristic zero, E=k(alpha), K=k(r,s), and
L=k(alpha,s), with r=alpha^48. The extension L/K is separable of degree48.
Consider the 48 trace operands c_0=1 and c_j=s*alpha^j for 1<=j<48.
Their trace pairing with the basis 1,alpha,...,alpha^47 has determinant

    -48^48 * r^47 * s^47,

so the unrestricted K-linear trace problem is invertible. Also the first
48 jets of 1,alpha,...,alpha^47 at alpha=1 have determinant product(j!).
These are exact full-rank statements.

The trace vector (0,1,0,...,0) is produced by

    f=1/(48*s*alpha).

But the intended contact form f d(alpha) is not closed on L:

    d(f d(alpha)) = -1/(48*s^2*alpha) ds wedge d(alpha) != 0.

For an allowed weight f in E, Tr(s*alpha*f) belongs to s*k(r), and so
cannot equal 1. Thus a separable finite trace algebra plus an invertible
48-jet matrix can still fail the closed-weight source problem. This is
a counterexample to that proposed abstract inference, not a counterexample
to the symmetric sextic or to its paper proof. The accompanying standard-
library script verifies all 2304 trace-pairing entries and the selected
trace vector exactly.

For a rational moving-point one-form Theta, the omitted term is explicit:

    d Tr(eta wedge Theta)
      = Tr(d eta wedge Theta - eta wedge d Theta).

The extra d eta term may not be dropped after solving an unrestricted
K(r,s)-linear trace equation.

6. A SMALL ACTUAL-TARGET CORRECTION THAT WOULD SUFFICE AFTER SOURCE MATCH
=====================================================================
For the weighted sextic G, the complete negative degree-six Jacobian
space has the 12 generators

    cG_a,dG_a,cG_b,dG_b,
    aG_c,bG_c,aG_d,bG_d,
    acG_u,adG_u,bcG_u,bdG_u.

Each is M(G) for a homogeneous vector field M whose only nonzero
coefficient is independent of its differentiation coordinate. Therefore
div(M)=0, directly. With the compatible weighted volume convention,

    d(i_M(Omega/G)) = -M(G) Omega/G^2.

Hence a source comparison proved modulo these 12 polynomial directions
has a concrete simple-pole correction. The missing assertion is that the
chosen traced source actually reduces to that finite polynomial source
space with controlled boundary terms. A pointwise jet comparison does
not establish that assertion. No denominator/support class is silently
discarded here.
