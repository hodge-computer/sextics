SCOPE OF THE REQUESTED CANONICAL TRACE COMPUTATION

No individual full normalized trace among the nine requested coefficient
functions has been evaluated in this subtask. No value has been inferred
from a single contact, sampled contacts, a sign orbit, or an incomplete
Groebner basis.

The nine equations in contact_forms_global_residue.json are CANDIDATE source
conditions for the specifically constructed eta_R = tau(R dh). They have
never been proved to hold. The exact regularity and nonzero-form certificates
prove that this is an available, nonzero closed form for the prescribed
source. They do not prove that its pushforward is that same source.

The public norm-contact argument itself distinguishes these statements:
the composition Gamma_* Gamma^* must be inverted on the relevant Hodge
sector. The actual eta_R used in the circuit has not been shown to include
that inverse. The affine moving-current formula also needs its exact source
comparison; cohomological support alone does not supply it. A nontrivial
constant combination of canonical forms or an exact differential correction
may be needed. A multiplier depending arbitrarily on r,s is not justified
by closedness.

ONE UNCONDITIONAL COMPUTED RELATION

Write the monic cubic as
  G=w^3+a(x)w^2+b(x)w+c(x),
and let a1,b2,c3 denote the leading coefficients of a,b,c. For this exact
target, all three depend only on (s,lambda). Define
  C1=2*b2^2*(d_s a1)-(a1*b2+3*c3)*(d_s b2)+2*b2*(d_s c3),
  C2=(2*a1*b2-6*c3)*(d_s a1)-a1^2*(d_s b2)+2*a1*(d_s c3).
The general-cubic source formula gives, before taking any trace,
  [x^4]M1=C1*alpha*U,
  [x^3]M2=C2*alpha*U.
Consequently the exact full trace relation
  C2*<[x^4]M1>-C1*<[x^3]M2>=0
is established. Here brackets mean the same normalized trace as in the
requested finite algebra. Both requested highest target coefficients are
zero, so these two candidate conditions reduce to the single moment
  m=<alpha*U>=0.
That moment's value remains uncomputed.

At lambda=s=1 the exact multipliers are
  C1=5773320/2197,
  C2=13396320/2197.
They are nonzero. The complete symbolic functions and verification are in
trace_top_moment.py and trace_top_moment.json. Reproduction:
  python trace_top_moment.py

INNER SELECTED-POINT ELIMINATION

With q=Q0+Qc*alpha^2 and
  h=E0+E2*alpha^2+alpha*(O1+O3*alpha^2),
put E=E0*Qc-E2*Q0 and O=O1*Qc-O3*Q0. Exact elimination gives
  Res_alpha(q,h)=D=Qc*E^2+Q0*O^2,
  alpha=-E/O on O != 0.
The rational differential identity
  (E/O)*(D_r/D)-2*E_r/O
    = [O*(E*Q0_r-2*Q0*E_r)+2*Q0*E*O_r]/D
is also checked. It reduces a prospective global-residue calculation on the
contact curve, but does not evaluate that residue. The O=0 summand and
compactification contributions cannot be dropped without proof.

WHY NO EULER-JACOBI ZERO IS CLAIMED

The usual affine Euler-Jacobi vanishing theorem requires control of zeros
at infinity. The present homogenized equations fail the simple hypothesis:
the point with all contact parameters zero, alpha nonzero, localization
variable zeta zero, and homogenizing coordinate zero satisfies every top
homogeneous equation. Thus a degree count cannot discard infinity residues.

Closedness of eta_R alone only implies closedness of its trace, not a zero
trace. Even if one establishes the expected compact holomorphic extension,
weighted moments such as Tr(alpha*eta_R) need additional pole control.
The monic chart u+Q1 can degenerate at the compactification boundary when
its u-coefficient vanishes. A limiting contact can then acquire components
over the projection's fixed locus. Excluding a quadratic root in the monic
chart does not exclude this boundary. The current work has not proved the
required cancellation there, and it is not used to assign any trace value.

The A/B sign symmetries were separately audited: they leave the actual
source trace operands invariant. They do not make the traces zero.
