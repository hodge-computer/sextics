1. Quotient-coefficient elimination is sound on the stated Delta open.

The builder represents the nineteen coefficients of g2 by numerators over
  d=(1-mu0*mu1)(1-2*mu0*mu1)(1-3*mu0*mu1).
Its recursive tridiagonal adjugate calculation checks exact divisibility
at each block and verifies all nineteen pivot equations. The determinant
of the original matrix has the same unit factors (with multiplicities).
Thus the substitution is unique when Delta != 0.

Dividing factors 1-k*mu0*mu1 out of the twenty-five remaining equations is
legitimate precisely because the builder requires --opens and adjoins their
inverses. It does not preserve the unsaturated ideal off that open, but no
such preservation is claimed.

2. Alpha elimination on bN != 0 preserves the incidence equations.

The identities recorded in contact_alpha_elimination.py give
  alpha=-aN/bN,
  q(alpha)=P/bN^2,
  h(alpha)=(a2*b0-a0*b2)*P/bN^3.
The converse follows from q2*h-(a2+alpha*b2)*q=aN+alpha*bN.
On P=0 with bN a unit, q2 is also a unit: q2=0 would imply q0=0 and hence
bN=0, a contradiction. Requiring aN to be a unit therefore exactly retains
the original alpha != 0 open on this summand.

The Jacobian determinant relation Je=q2*bN*J has the correct convention.
The alternative equation G3 satisfies
  P-G3=sum_j H_weight_j*H_j.
This proves equality of the two coefficient ideals. Their Jacobian
DETERMINANTS agree on H=0: the last rows of the matrices can differ by a
linear combination of the first forty-four rows. A statement that the
Jacobian matrices themselves agree would be too strong.

3. The current modular system is not yet the full requested trace algebra.

The builders currently include Delta and alpha (or its aN replacement),
and the additional bN open when alpha is eliminated. They omit the
incidence Jacobian, the selected degree-nine residue-dual determinant,
the degree-ten regularity determinant, beta, and G_w at the point. Those
opens are part of the requested full finite-etale algebra. Consequently a
completed basis for the current larger specialized ideal would still need
the remaining localizations before it could supply the requested traces.
The receipts correctly mark full_regularity_localization_included=false
and traces_computed=false.

4. The exceptional branches are projection/emptiness experiments.

On the bN=0, q2-unit branch the builder retains aN=bN=0 and q2 != 0, but
omits alpha and its quadratic equation q0+q2*alpha^2=0. The original alpha
unit would additionally force q0 != 0. This projected parameter algebra
is not yet the original branch algebra or its trace algebra.

On the q2=0 branch the builder substitutes q0=q2=0 and omits alpha. The
original cubic h(alpha)=0, the alpha unit and other source opens must be
restored to obtain that branch's actual algebra. Showing the larger
projected parameter ideal empty would safely show this branch empty;
a nonempty basis or its dimension does not yet compute the branch degree
or trace.

5. Scope of modular experiments.

Specialization to fixed (lambda,r,s) and a finite characteristic is an
experiment until flatness/good reduction and the full localization are
verified. A timed-out or incomplete F4/Buchberger run proves no quotient
degree or trace value. No data error was found in the actual pivot
elimination or bN-open algebra identities during this read-only audit.
