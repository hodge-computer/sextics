1. AN EXACT RELATION BETWEEN TWO REQUESTED TRACES
================================================
Write T14=<[x^4]M1>, T23=<[x^3]M2>, and m=<alpha*U>, where brackets denote
the normalized trace of the FULL localized finite algebra over Q(lambda,r,s).
An exact expansion of the universal source coefficients gives

  T14=C1*m,                    T23=C2*m,
  C1=2*b2^2*(a1)_s-(a1*b2+3*c3)*(b2)_s+2*b2*(c3)_s,
  C2=(2*a1*b2-6*c3)*(a1)_s-a1^2*(b2)_s+2*a1*(c3)_s.

Here a1,b2,c3 are the highest x-coefficients of the protected cubic's
a(x),b(x),c(x). The actual rational expressions are recorded in
research_trace/trace_top_moment.json.

The multipliers, evaluated at lambda=s=1, are

  C1=5773320/2197,             C2=13396320/2197.

These are multiplier values, NOT trace values. The unconditional trace
relation is C2*T14-C1*T23=0. Both proposed highest-coefficient targets are
zero, so their two tests reduce to the single unknown test m=0.
No value, including zero, has been established for m. The seven other
individual coefficient traces also remain unevaluated.

See research_trace/trace_scope.txt for the missing inverse-map step and
the boundary contributions that prevent an unproved Euler-Jacobi cancellation.

2. EXACT COMPUTATIONAL REDUCTIONS
=================================
Quartic elimination. The 19 coefficients of g2 are eliminated on the original
Delta open. For z=mu0*mu1, a common denominator is

  D=(1-z)*(1-2*z)*(1-3*z).

The independent audit works over QQ[mu0,...,mu4,R0,...,R43], with every sextic
coefficient independent. It reconstructs 19 integral polynomial numerators
(426 terms in total) and checks all 19 pivot coefficients identically. The
remaining 25 coefficient equations are equivalent on D!=0. The modular builder
was also compared over F101 with all 49 variables symbolic, not sampled.
This is exact CAS algebra, not a Lean formalization.

Point elimination. Write

  q=q0+q2*alpha^2,
  h=a0+a2*alpha^2+alpha*(b0+b2*alpha^2),
  aN=q2*a0-q0*a2,              bN=q2*b0-q0*b2,
  P=q2*aN^2+q0*bN^2.

On bN!=0, the equations q=h=0 are equivalent to

  P=0,                        alpha=-aN/bN.

The eliminated Jacobian determinant is q2*bN times the original determinant.
An alternative last equation G3 depends only on rho and mu; the packet gives
the exact ideal witness P-G3=sum_j w_j*H_j. The Jacobian determinants agree
on H=0; the matrices need not be identical. The bN=0 summand is retained as
an outstanding obligation and has not been declared empty.

Sign symmetries. The two actual involutions flip (A,alpha) or (B,alpha).
An exact character audit checks 18,336 coefficient slots and shows that all
nine source expressions are invariant. Their normalized traces descend to
the invariant algebra, whose degree is four times smaller. This symmetry
does not cancel those source expressions or determine the remaining traces.

3. ACTUAL SOLVER EXPERIMENTS AND THEIR LIMITS
===========================================
research_trace/finite_computation_status.json and the per-run receipts/logs record
the completed experiments. Native FLINT bounded Buchberger and portable
msolve F4 were run on specialized equations. Each run records its input,
parameters, resource budget, exit status, and available progress.

The modular inputs are PRELIMINARY larger algebras. Runs using --opens or
the recorded F4 saturation include Delta and alpha (or their exact counterparts
after elimination); the raw runs omit those opens as well. None yet includes
all of J, the selected degree-nine and degree-ten determinants, beta, and G_w
from the original finite-algebra localization. The bN=0 and q2=0 screens
additionally project away their alpha fibers. Consequently,
even a completed basis for one of these preliminary systems would require
further localization, branch accounting, and generic-specialization proof
before it could certify one of the requested characteristic-zero traces.

Incomplete bases and failed solver processes have not been assigned a
quotient degree or used as evidence of a trace identity.

finite_target_rhs.json contains the required right-hand-side coefficients at
(lambda,r,s)=(1,2,3), over Q and modulo37. Those target values are not the
unknown source traces; they are retained only as comparison data.

A separate 600-digit integer-relation probe of five coordinates of the
certified contact found no candidates at the tested degrees 4,8,12,16,20
and coefficient bound 10^16. This bounded discovery search is not a proof
of a lower bound on the field degree and is not a trace calculation.
