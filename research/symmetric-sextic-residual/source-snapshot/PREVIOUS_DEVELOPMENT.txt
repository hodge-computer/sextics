SYMMETRIC SEXTIC: CHECKED PARTIAL FORMALIZATION, SECOND DEVELOPMENT
6 November 2026

STATUS

This is an unfinished attempt to prove Ben Diamond's mathematical result for
symmetric-sextic-residual in hodge-computer/sextics.

THE FULL TARGET IS NOT PROVED:
  HodgeComputer.HasCertificate HodgeSubmission.F HodgeSubmission.q HodgeSubmission.A

There is no Submission.certificate, hidden primitive hypothesis, replacement
verifier, or declaration of the desired result as an axiom in this package.
The files provide substantial checked prerequisites and a separately labelled
record of direct certificate research. They are not an accepted solution.

Compared with the first development, this adds a complete proof of the exact
sextic's Jacobian nonsingularity condition, formal polynomial and quotient-ring
semantics for the two finite coefficient matrices, and exhaustive orbit
representative coverage. It also records attempts at an actual rational
primitive. None produced the required primitive.

1. EXACT TARGET AND JACOBIAN NONSINGULARITY

Write X=(X0,X1,X2), Y=(Y0,Y1,Y2),
  QX=X0X1+X0X2+X1X2,
  QY=Y0Y1+Y0Y2+Y1Y2.
The exact protected polynomials are
  F=sum Xi^6 + sum Yi^6 + (5/7)QX^2 QY + (10/7)QX QY^2,
  A=X0X1X2Y0Y1Y2,
and q=1. The unequal coupling coefficients mean that exchanging the entire
X and Y blocks is NOT a symmetry.

HodgeTarget.lean is byte-identical to output of the pinned repository's
trusted target_source generator for the protected instance. Its SHA256 is
  a51532e0442d72e8ce95d6728390fac0f719287d1cf20020701625bc1b5409d9.
SymmetricReflections.lean binds the expanded target to the compact formulas,
proves the valid coordinate-swap and Y-parity identities, and verifies the
change X=(a+s,a-s,b), Y=(c+t,c-t,d) and its inverse identities.

NEW: SymmetricSmoothness.lean proves

  SymmetricSmoothness.gradient_vanishes_only_at_origin
    (x : Fin 6 -> C)
    (hx : forall i, eval x (pderiv i HodgeSubmission.F) = 0) : x = 0

and equivalently

  SymmetricSmoothness.nonzero_point_has_nonzero_partial
    (x : Fin 6 -> C) (hx : x != 0) :
    exists i, eval x (pderiv i HodgeSubmission.F) != 0.

The code uses Lean's usual Unicode notation for these statements. It also
proves target_homogeneous, target_ne_zero, target_totalDegree (=6), and
source_homogeneous for the exact generated F and A.

Proof: G=7F is integral and reduces modulo 5 to 2 times the Fermat sextic.
Every first partial therefore reduces to 2 Xi^5. Any degree-25 monomial in
six variables contains some Xi^5. Choose one such index for each monomial
and multiply the corresponding partial by the complementary degree-20
monomial. The square coefficient matrix of these products reduces to 2I,
so its integer determinant is nonzero and stays nonzero in any field of
characteristic zero. At a common zero of the partials, the vector of all
degree-25 monomials annihilates this invertible matrix. Hence every Xi^25,
and therefore every Xi, vanishes.

Mod5Smoothness.lean proves this argument uniformly. It never materializes
the 142506-by-142506 matrix. The determinant reduction is symbolic. In
particular, the proof does not use a nonexistent embedding from F5 to C.

Scope: this is the exact algebraic projective Jacobian nonsingularity
condition. It is not a new Scheme.Smooth statement, an irreducibility
formalization, or a certificate theorem.

2. FINITE ALGEBRA AND THE MATRIX SEMANTICS

The older geometric argument's finite calculation uses
  R=Q[z0,z1,z2,z3,z4,u]/(z0^5,z1^5,z2^5,z3^5,z4^5,u^2)
with weights (1,1,1,1,1,2) and involution swapping 0/1 and 2/3.
Put
  L=z0+z1+z2+z3+z4,
  D=z0-z1+z2-z3,
  H=z0+z1+z2+z3,
  Q=D(L^2+u)^5.

The earlier modules prove ranks 82 and 74 for integer arrays of sizes
120-by-82 and 74-by-82, mapped to Q, and kernel dimension 8 for the second
array. Selected square minors have explicitly checked inverses modulo 101;
the general rank arguments lift nonzero integer determinants to Q.
Every inverse-product and integer-array binding is checked by the kernel.

NEW: the polynomial-to-matrix association is now a Lean theorem.
WeightedPolynomialModel.lean proves exact coefficient formulas for genuine
MvPolynomial expressions using the multinomial theorem. Twenty small row
modules check all 194 rows, covering 15908 coefficients. The combined
WeightedPolynomialBindings.lean proves equality with the earlier arrays.

WeightedPolynomialQuotient.lean proves bounded coefficients vanish on the
truncation ideal and therefore define well-defined quotient coordinates.
It proves Q=D L^10+5u D L^8 in the quotient by an explicit remainder divisible
by u^2. WeightedPolynomialRational.lean works directly in the Q quotient.

The strongest rank endpoints, in namespace WeightedSupport.PolynomialModel,
are
  Q_rational_quotient_matrix_eq
  HQ_rational_quotient_matrix_eq
  Q_rational_quotient_rank : QRationalQuotientMatrix.rank = 82
  HQ_rational_quotient_rank : HQRationalQuotientMatrix.rank = 74.

Their entries are coefficients of actual quotient-ring products by the
original Q=D(L^2+u)^5 and HQ. They do not assume that the displayed integer
arrays are the right polynomial matrices.

NEW: WeightedPolynomialOrbits, the three OrbitBlocks modules,
WeightedPolynomialOrbitEnumeration, and WeightedPolynomialOrbitCompleteness
prove exhaustive representative coverage among all 6250 capped monomials.
The final theorems source_orbit_cover, q_target_orbit_cover and
hq_target_orbit_cover apply to arbitrary capped exponent vectors of the
appropriate weighted degrees. The symmetry module proves the actual
variable-renaming and plus/minus identities.

PRECISE REMAINING ALGEBRAIC SCOPE:
The coordinate matrices, quotient products, ranks, and full representative
coverage are proved. We have not yet built the abstract graded eigenspaces
and their coordinate LinearEquiv objects. The remaining normal-form and
interpolation lemmas must prove that capped coefficients determine the
quotient class, that the orbit sums give inverse coordinate maps, and that
multiplication commutes with those maps. Therefore this package does not
claim a completed Lean theorem of injectivity/surjectivity for abstract
multiplication maps R5+ -> R16- and R5+ -> R17-. It also does not identify
these spaces with geometric Jacobian/Hodge groups.

The independent Python matrix audit is retained as a separate check. It
reconstructs all entries and minors without importing the upstream matrix
generator. This Python check is no longer the only link between the literal
arrays and actual polynomial products.

3. EARLIER ABSTRACT MONODROMY LEMMA

ProjectorCore.lean proves that a subspace stable under specified nontrivial
transvections and direction-transport maps is zero or the whole space,
provided the pairing has zero left radical and the directions span.

Its strongest theorem is
  WeightedSexticMonodromy.invariant_submodule_eq_bot_or_top.
All hypotheses are explicit. It does not establish that the actual family
satisfies those hypotheses or construct a geometric projector. The newer
PR219 uses a different geometric route, so this module is retained as a
proved ingredient of the earlier PR218 argument, not as its replacement.

4. THE EXACT MISSING CERTIFICATE INPUT

A direct route is available in the existing pinned Lean library. On the
hypersurface function field K, choose g=partial_0 F and tangent derivations
  E_i=partial_(i+1) - (partial_(i+1)F/g) partial_0.
It suffices to produce rational functions u_i, possibly in a finite
extension of K, satisfying

  sum_i E_i(u_i) = (A partial_0^2 F - (partial_0 A)g)/g^3.

The existing lemmas
  HoldoutConstruction.finite_residue_solution_clearing_canonical
  HoldoutConstruction.hasCertificate_of_normalized
handle the subsequent algebraic construction when their hypotheses are
supplied. No actual u_i satisfying this equation was found in this work.

The certificate itself requires a nonzero homogeneous denominator D, legal
coprimality with F, suitably homogeneous N_i, and a polynomial Q with

  A D^2 - D sum_i(N_i partial_i F)
  + F (D div(N) - sum_i(N_i partial_i D)) = F^2 Q.

A nonzero Q is allowed. There is no arbitrary denominator-degree bound in
the target. An existential nonconstructive proof is also permitted. Neither
printing explicit polynomials nor avoiding Classical.choice is required.
The obstacle is the missing proof of existence, not the use of classical
logic in Lean.

5. DIRECT RESEARCH: NOT LEAN PROOFS

The research/ directory records the actual attempted direct construction.
These files are supplementary calculations and audits; none supplies a
certificate or is imported as an axiom into Lean.

(a) Symmetry-compressed denominator searches
Fixed invariant denominators and relative-character denominators were
screened against both normalized equations, retaining all numerator kernel
corrections in the complete degree-six search. The degree-six screen
exhausted 1464 projective denominator choices over F11 and 52060 over F37.
The only successful denominator class in those complete screens was
proportional to F, which is illegal. A known Fermat certificate is a positive
control. No legal hit occurred in the recorded candidate screens.

These are bounded modular searches, not a proof of nonexistence for the
original problem. Rank-deficient modular inconsistency alone must not be
promoted to a characteristic-zero obstruction. The fixed Vandermonde
product denominator has a stronger full-column-rank obstruction, but this
does not exclude its powers or arbitrary other supports. The auxiliary
annihilator JSON uses a selected numerator section; the complete sextic
screen is the version retaining every first-equation kernel correction.

(b) Explicit conic and genus-three source calculation
DirectCertificateResearch.txt and gauss_manin_scope.txt give the derivation.
Elementary symmetric coordinates reduce the invariant equation to a conic,
but the original source retains the Vandermonde character. The resulting
cover is a genus-three hyperelliptic curve. The actual double-pole density,
including the derivative of the branch factor, was computed. A rank-six
Gauss-Manin source/connection circuit retains all three base directions.

The circuit and exact-fiber scripts check generic symbolic identities,
100 exact F101 specializations, a full Q(i) replay, and zero curvature at
that characteristic-zero fiber. Compact primitive ansatzes with numerator
degree <=3 and denominator 1 or the discriminant did not succeed. They do
not cover arbitrary rational functions or extra logarithmic classes at
moving horizontal poles.

A separate exact pole argument rules out a fiber-only primitive on one
specified fiber. It does not rule out the full three-direction primitive.
The full matrices, normalization details, determinant witness, and limited
scope are recorded. No result here is a Lean nonexistence theorem.

(c) New PR219 and its discussion packet
PR219 uses genus-four norm contacts and a 48-dimensional finite-jet span
calculation. This simplifies the geometric argument, but does not supply an
exact rational source identity for our protected target. Its stated current
formula is for a cyclic cubic; our weighted quotient has lower cubic terms
and requires additional work.

The public packet inspected was
  https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/discussions/120#discussioncomment-19448881
  https://github.com/user-attachments/files/34499077/sextics-discussion-packet-2026-11-06.zip
The packet concerns four-plus-two-residual, not symmetric-sextic-residual.
One Lean file checks a literal 48-by-48 inverse; the other proves a cyclic
suspension conversion conditional on a supplied twisted core primitive.
The packet explicitly leaves the exact trace/source calculation open.
It cannot be used as a complete proof of this target. Those third-party
sources are linked here, not included among our checked modules.

6. VALIDATION AND REPRODUCTION

modules.json gives the dependency order of all 44 Lean modules. Every source
was compiled against the pins above. The ten earlier modules were separately
kernel-replayed in the first development; all 34 added modules were replayed
in fresh leanchecker processes in this development. The prior and current
receipts retain exact source/object hashes and logs. The aggregate receipt
checks that every packaged source agrees with its corresponding successful
replay record. The axiom audit asks Lean for every theorem's dependencies;
all 722 reports contain only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound or a
smaller set. No source has sorry, admit, native_decide, a new axiom, or an
unsafe declaration.

Kernel replay here means Lean's leanchecker checks each module into its
imported environment. It is not a second independent kernel implementation,
and it does not rebuild all of Mathlib from an empty environment.

The archive includes sources, receipts, logs, and research scripts. Compiled
objects, the toolchain, and the Mathlib cache are omitted. To reproduce,
install the pinned Lean toolchain and use a checkout at the pinned commit.
From the repository's formal/lean directory, obtain/build the required
imports, then run

  lake exe cache get
  lake build HodgeComputer.Verify Mathlib.Tactic
  lake env python3 /absolute/path/to/package/verify_partial.py --repo /absolute/path/to/sextics
  lake env python3 /absolute/path/to/package/audit_axioms.py --repo /absolute/path/to/sextics
  python3 /absolute/path/to/package/independent_matrix_audit.py

Additional non-tactic Mathlib imports are listed at the tops of the sources;
they must have compiled objects. The pinned cache used here provided them.
verify_partial.py compiles and then separately replays every module, one
process at a time, with one Lean worker and a 4096 MiB compile allocation
limit. Its optional --lock-file flag permits safe serialization with other
Lean processes. It validates the repository, Mathlib and Lean pins.

The independent matrix audit uses Python's standard library. The research
scripts additionally use SymPy and, for the complete finite-field screen,
NumPy. They are not required to check any Lean theorem. Do not interpret
research script success as success of the full certificate target.

This execution environment had a PID-namespace/procfs discrepancy: Lean's
/proc/<own-pid>/exe lookup failed while /proc/self/exe worked. The included
runtime_compat/own_exe_path.c redirects only that exact self-executable
lookup. It changes no proof/kernel functions and grants no privileges.
Normal environments do not need it. The official toolchain archive SHA256
verified before execution was
  890afd185370f85666025b883914ab4f4b339136f8c96167b69cfb62aecaf235.

WHAT IS STILL REQUIRED

The finite quotient eigenspace coordinate isomorphisms remain to be packaged
formally. Beyond them, the geometric correspondence, support and
specialization arguments and their passage to the exact target have not
been formalized. The alternative direct route still needs the displayed
rational primitive identity, including any new horizontal-pole classes and
necessary coordinate corrections.

These are substantive missing proof obligations. This package is a checked
partial development, not a proof that the whole informal argument is valid
and not a valid solution submission. No external repository, discussion,
proposal, or message was modified.
