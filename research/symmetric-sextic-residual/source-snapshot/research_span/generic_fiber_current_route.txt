1. THE SPECIFIC THEOREM STILL NEEDED

Let k be a characteristic-zero field admitting an embedding into C. Let

  T = {G=0} in P(1,1,1,1,2),
  sigma(a,b,c,d,u) = (a,b,-c,-d,u),
  V = H^3_dR(T/k)^-,

where T is smooth, the weighted vertex is absent, and H^{3,0}(T)^-=0.
Suppose an algebraic contact-parameter curve has smooth general member,
normal cohomology h^0(N)=h^1(N)=1, and the actual infinitesimal Abel--Jacobi
functional, identified through the normal-obstruction residue formula, has
coefficient rows spanning the 48-dimensional dual of R_6(G)^-.

Then the desired conclusion is the zero restriction map

  V --> H^3(Omega^bullet_{k(T)/k}).                         (GF)

Equivalently, every rational three-form representing the restriction of a
class in V is d_rel of a rational two-form. Allowing a finite extension of k
to define the contact component does not weaken (GF): normalized field trace
descends a primitive. This is the missing "contact obstruction span implies
negative rational de Rham exactness" theorem. It is not presently an existing
theorem/type in the pinned Lean development. No axiom with this statement has
been introduced.

2. WHY THE NEW SINGLE-FIBER CALCULATION CAN REACH THE GENERIC PENCIL

A conclusion only about lambda=1 does not imply a statement about the
fourfold. The following steps are essential.

(a) Regard the 44 exact contact equations as equations in lambda and the
    45 contact coefficients. Fix rho0=1/4. The certified nonzero 44 by 44
    Jacobian at lambda=1 makes the local component with rho0 fixed etale over
    the lambda-line. Its generic point is therefore defined over a finite
    extension K of a number field k0(lambda). The isolated contact point at
    lambda=1 is algebraic: all its defining equations have rational
    coefficients and its local fiber is zero-dimensional.

(b) Unfix rho0. Over K the contact component is a curve, smooth at the
    selected K-point. Its Taylor coefficients in rho0 are rational functions
    of the equation coefficients and the inverse contact Jacobian. The
    selected obstruction minor is likewise a regular rational expression
    after inverting the displayed nonzero minors. Its nonzero specialization
    at lambda=1 therefore implies that it is nonzero over K. This step must
    use the equations with variable lambda, not treat the specialized
    equation as a constant family.

(c) Smoothness of T, smoothness of the contact curve, regularity of the
    residue sequence, and nonzero normalization also persist after removing
    their algebraic zero loci. The exact 60-12=48 Jacobian calculation
    identifies the relevant quotient at lambda=1; smooth weighted residue
    theory identifies its constant rank along the resulting open pencil.

(d) Complete and normalize the contact-parameter curve over K, and take the
    closure of its universal family. After a further finite extension if
    necessary, this gives a smooth projective parameter curve Lambda and a
    correspondence Gamma in Lambda x T. A formal jet itself is not Lambda,
    and the genus-four contact fiber is not the parameter curve. Its image
    in T has dimension at most two. Replace Gamma by its negative projection
    (Gamma - sigma Gamma)/2; the image stays in a proper divisor.

Thus one obtains an actual correspondence on the generic weighted fiber.
No assertion about every special weighted sextic is required. Completing
the parameter curve and extending the correspondence are algebraic-geometry
steps not supplied merely by a nonzero determinant.

3. PROOF OF (GF), GIVEN THE UNFORMALIZED COMPARISON THEOREMS

Choose an embedding of K into C. This sends lambda to a transcendental value
over the coefficient number field; it is not specialization to lambda=1.
All nonzero algebraic minors above remain nonzero under this embedding.

The normal sequence and Serre duality identify the computed functional with
the differential of Gamma^*: H^{2,1}(T)^- -> H^{1,0}(Lambda). A class killed
by Gamma^* is killed by every Taylor coefficient. The full-rank jet minor
therefore makes this map injective. Multiplication by a common nonzero
meromorphic scalar does not change its kernel; a finite jet determinant
alone does not establish the needed identification or scalar.

An algebraic correspondence acts on rational Betti cohomology and preserves
Hodge structures. Hence injectivity also holds on the conjugate (1,2) part.
There are no (3,0) or (0,3) classes in the negative sector. Poincare duality
and transposition then give

  Gamma_*: H^1(Lambda)(-1) --> H^3(T)^-

surjectively. No claim that Gamma_* Gamma^* is the identity is necessary.
Positivity and a polynomial inverse are also unnecessary for this particular
surjectivity-to-support argument.

The image of Gamma_* is supported on the divisor swept by the curves.
Functoriality with supports makes its restriction to the complement zero.
Algebraic de Rham comparison transfers this zero map from Betti cohomology
to algebraic de Rham cohomology. The correspondence and its support were
defined over K, so extension-of-scalars injectivity descends the zero map
back to K. Restrict further to a smooth affine open avoiding the support
and the poles of the chosen representative. On an affine smooth variety,
de Rham cohomology is computed by regular differential forms. Zero class
there means an actual regular two-form primitive there, hence a rational
two-form over K(T). This proves (GF), not merely a numerical pairing zero.

Finally, for finite K/k0(lambda), trace on differentials commutes with d_rel.
If d_rel beta=omega and omega is defined over k0(lambda), then

  d_rel(Tr(beta)/[K:k0(lambda)]) = omega.

This explicit descent is what permits the generic-fiber statement to be
used for the original rational pencil.

4. FROM THE RELATIVE PRIMITIVE TO THE EXACT PROTECTED SOURCE

The actual affine chart, already bound to HodgeSubmission.F/A in
proof_attempt3/SymmetricCubicModel.lean, is

  X=(1+v,1-v,r),  Y=(z+lambda v,z-lambda v,s z),
  w=v^2,  G_raw=ell(lambda) G,
  ell=(14 lambda^6 - 10 lambda^4 - 5 lambda^2 + 14)/7.

Before setting the scale a=1, the complete six-coordinate Jacobian in
(a,r,z,s,v,lambda) is 4 a^5 v z; this sign and scale have been proved in Lean.
On the weighted quotient use coordinates (a,b,c,d,u), with
(a,b,c,d,u)=(1,r,z,s z,w). The required weighted degree-six source is

  R_lambda = 2 b d (a^2-u)(c^2-lambda^2 u) / ell(lambda)^2.       (S)

The factor 2 is the remaining Jacobian factor after w=v^2. On a=1 the
ambient weighted volume is db wedge dc wedge dd wedge du. With the stated
coordinate ordering, the pulled ambient protected five-form is

  d lambda wedge [R_lambda Omega_weighted / G^2].

This is a differential-form identity still to be bound formally; the
polynomial evaluations and full Jacobian alone are not that binding.
Taking the compatible residue yields d lambda wedge omega_R, where omega_R
represents the negative relative de Rham class of Res(R_lambda Omega/G^2).
The sign of the Poincare residue convention must be fixed consistently.

On G_u != 0, double-pole reduction gives, up to that single global residue
sign, the rational three-form

  omega_R = (G_u R_u - G_uu R) / G_u^3 * db wedge dc wedge dd.

This follows by differentiating (R/G_u)/G before taking a simple residue.
It is the source that must match the tangent-derivation equation. Replacing
R by an arbitrary scalar multiple, or replacing G_raw by monic G without
the ell^-2 factor, changes the target.

If (GF) supplies d_rel beta=omega_R, the absolute rational primitive is

  gamma = - d lambda wedge beta,
  d gamma = d lambda wedge omega_R.

Dependence of beta on lambda creates no extra term because d lambda wedge
d lambda=0. This avoids relative Leray and any integration in lambda. One
must still pull the primitive back through the finite reflection chart,
express it in the canonical tangent-derivation basis, and prove the exact
normal-residue equation required by FiniteResidueClearing. Nonzero elements
of the hypersurface function field must be lifted through polynomials not
divisible by F; arbitrary ambient fractions with poles along F are illegal.
The existing Prime F proof, finite-extension trace, and regular/Euler
clearing can then assemble HasCertificate. They do not create beta.
