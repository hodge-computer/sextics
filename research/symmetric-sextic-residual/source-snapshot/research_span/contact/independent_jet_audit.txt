2. CONTACT COEFFICIENT INDUCTION

The perturbation majorant is coefficientwise nonnegative:

  sum_d s_d ((M(t)+E(t))^d - M(t)^d),

where M bounds approximate coordinate coefficients, E bounds their errors,
and s_d bounds absolute polynomial coefficients of degree d. It correctly
majorizes every contact equation's evaluation error.

At stage n, E_n has not yet been assigned. Compare the true series with a
hybrid series whose coefficients below n are true but whose nth coefficient
is the approximation. The hybrid residual is bounded by the displayed
majorant plus the exact approximate residual. The difference between the
true nth coefficient and its approximation enters linearly through J(x0),

error multiplying the approximate nth coefficient; it is not mistakenly
using only the approximate constant Jacobian.

Consequently, subject to the ordinary implicit-function/formal-series
theorem, these are bounds on the coefficients of the genuine contact
branch. The checker is not differentiating inside a truncated Artinian
ring and is not asserting that such differentiation descends to that ring.
