7. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLOSE
==========================
Injectivity/surjectivity of the cohomological correspondence does not assert
that the particular form eta_R=tau(R dh) pushes forward to R itself. In a
literal trace construction an inverse of the push-pull operator can be
required. The previously proposed nine
literal trace identities remain unevaluated, and none is asserted here.

To finish through this route, one must either formalize the geometric pairing
and the supported-correspondence-to-rational-de-Rham-exactness argument, or
construct and verify the corresponding literal rational primitive together
with its exact differential correction. The generic-fiber route in
../generic_fiber_current_route.txt avoids constructing a push-pull inverse:
surjectivity suffices to prove that restriction of the negative classes to
the function field is zero. The earlier automatic legal-clearing theorem
then removes the polynomial-witness and denominator-clearing burden.

The algebraic differentiation/annihilation lemmas, actual target symmetry and
primality, and automatic clearing have Lean proofs. The new finite contraction,
smoothness, and48-jet witness are exact computer-assisted mathematical proofs
with independent rational replay, but are not Lean-checked witnesses. This
packet does not claim an unconditional RegularGameExists or HasCertificate.
