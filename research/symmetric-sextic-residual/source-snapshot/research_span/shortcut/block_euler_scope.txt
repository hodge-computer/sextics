BLOCK-EULER SUPPORT: STATUS AND PRESCRIBED-SUPPORT CORRECTION
==========================================================

Protected target
----------------
The target is symmetric-sextic-residual, q=1, with its unchanged coefficients
5/7 and 10/7. Write U=sum Xi^6, V=sum Yi^6, q=e2(X), Q=e2(Y),
A=e3(X)e3(Y), and G=7F=7(U+V)+5q^2Q+10qQ^2. The symbol q=e2(X)
in these displayed polynomial formulas is distinct from the pole parameter.

The X-block Euler support is

  D_X=E_X(G)=42U+20q^2Q+20qQ^2.

It is nonzero, homogeneous of degree six, independently S3-invariant in both
blocks, and Y-even. It is legal. Indeed F is prime (proved for the actual
target in SymmetricPrime.lean). Divisibility F | D_X would, by the equal
degrees, make D_X a scalar multiple of F. Comparing the pure Y_i^6 and
X_i^6 coefficients contradicts that possibility.

All nonradial linear combinations of the two block Euler denominators have
the same support on F=0, since

  E_Y(G)=6G-E_X(G),
  E_X(G)+c E_Y(G)=(1-c)D_X+6cG.

The case c=1 is proportional to G and illegal. In particular, the earlier
E_X-2E_Y near-current does not provide a new support.

Earlier exact obstruction and its consequence for EVERY power
------------------------------------------------------------
The independent exact characteristic-zero degree-six calculation in
proof_attempt3/independent_degree6_audit.py and norm_support_scope.txt
excludes every invariant Y-even degree-six denominator other than a multiple
of F. It includes the complete equivariant numerator module, every first-
equation kernel correction, and the ordinary modulo-F divergence condition.

The prescribed-support theorem in sextics/docs/01-certificates.md, section
"Prescribed support and pole multiplicity", says that at pole parameter one,
existence with poles supported on V(D) is equivalent to existence with the
prescribed denominator D. Its proof expressly permits singular supports;
it uses filtered mixed Hodge modules and projective strictness. This theorem
has NOT been formalized in the current Lean packet.

Apply that theorem to D_X. A hypothetical certificate with denominator
D_X^m, for any m>=1, would give a certificate with denominator D_X.
Averaging over S3(X) x S3(Y) preserves this invariant denominator and the
target. Averaging under Y -> -Y gives the required numerator parity.
The ordinary-to-normalized Euler correction retains D_X. This would lie in
the complete degree-six system already excluded over characteristic zero.

Therefore ALL powers of this support are already excluded by the earlier
exact obstruction combined with the paper's prescribed-support theorem.
This is a mathematical consequence with an explicitly external geometric
input, not a newly completed Lean obstruction.

Redundant modular tests actually performed before noticing this consequence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The new script block_euler_fixed_support.py reconstructs the complete free
equivariant E0,E1,E2 bases independently of the earlier polynomial routines.
It solves both equations together over F_37:

  N(G)=A D,   D div(N)-N(D)=0 modulo G.

The ambient, rather than invariant-coordinate, divergence is used. The
second equation permits an arbitrary multiple of G. Converting a solution
for G to F multiplies the numerator vector by seven.

  support   denominator degree  columns  rows   coefficient rank  augmented rank
  D_X^2     12                  1206     2648   1075              1076
  D_X^3     18                  5276     10607  3988              3989

Thus both systems are inconsistent over F_37. The power-two run took less
than one second. The power-three run took 26.3 seconds and peaked at
704668 KiB resident memory under a 1536 MiB address-space cap. It had already
finished when the prescribed-support correction arrived. No power-four
computation was run; its raw dense matrix would require 3.67 GiB.

These modular failures alone are NOT characteristic-zero obstructions:
their coefficient matrices have nontrivial kernels, and an independent
characteristic-zero upper-rank bound was not established here. They are
redundant evidence only. The all-powers conclusion above comes instead from
the earlier exact degree-six result and the explicitly cited paper theorem.

No current or certificate was constructed. No protected source was edited.
