1. EXACT CONTACT EXISTENCE
==========================
Use X=(a+z,a-z,b), Y=(c+lambda*z,c-lambda*z,d), u=z^2. The monic quotient is

Contact data comprise 45 coefficients: five in Q1, five free coefficients
in Q2=ab+Q2', eight in each parity cubic Aplus,Bminus, and nineteen in an
invariant quartic g2. The 44 coefficient equations are exactly

 h6=Q1^3-h2*Q1^2+h4*Q1+Aplus^2-Bminus^2+Q2*g2.

They imply the complete norm presentation

     +Q2*g2+Aplus^2-Bminus^2.

At lambda=1, fix the a^2 coefficient rho0 of Q1 to 1/4, add r,alpha,
set s=1, and impose Q2(1,r,alpha,alpha)=(Aplus+Bminus)(1,r,alpha,alpha)=0.
There are 46 equations in 46 remaining unknowns.

Numerical homotopy discovered a candidate. Exact rational checks then certify
an actual root; no floating-point residual or singular value is used as proof.

theorem supplies a unique exact zero in the ball. The inverse-defect bound
