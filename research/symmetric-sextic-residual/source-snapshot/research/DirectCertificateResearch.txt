# Exact conic reduction and a three-direction Gauss–Manin attempt

## Status and scope

No full certificate has been obtained. This work preserves the exact symmetric
sextic with coefficients 5/7 and 10/7 and source
`A=X0*X1*X2*Y0*Y1*Y2`. It supplies an explicit source and connection circuit,
an exhaustive obstruction to a primitive on one fixed conic fiber, and a
bounded search that retains all three projective base directions.

These are exact algebra calculations and mathematical arguments, not new Lean
theorems. `DirectCertificate.lean` was deliberately not created: there is no
unconditional certificate to put in it. The previously compiled
`SymmetricReflections.lean` remains separate.

The block exchange is **not** a symmetry: it exchanges the coefficients 5/7
and 10/7. No block-exchange restriction is imposed below.

## 1. The exact symmetric quotient and its branch character

Use elementary symmetric coordinates

\[
p=e_1(X),\quad q=e_2(X),\quad r=e_3(X),\qquad
P=e_1(Y),\quad Q=e_2(Y),\quad R=e_3(Y).
\]

Newton's identity is

\[
\sum X_i^6=p^6-6p^4q+9p^2q^2-2q^3+6p^3r-12pqr+3r^2.
\]

Set

\[
a=p^3-2pq,\quad b=P^3-2PQ,
\]

\[
J(p,q)=-2p^6+6p^4q-3p^2q^2-2q^3,
\]

\[
k=-\frac13\left[J(p,q)+J(P,Q)+\frac57q^2Q+\frac{10}{7}qQ^2\right].
\]

Then the protected sextic is exactly

\[
F=3\bigl((r+a)^2+(R+b)^2-k\bigr),\qquad A=rR.
\]

The invariant quotient alone is a conic. It is insufficient to discard its
branch factors. The two symmetric-coordinate Jacobians are Vandermonde
cubics, with squares

\[
D_X=c_X+l_Xr-27r^2,\quad
c_X=p^2q^2-4q^3,\quad l_X=-4p^3+18pq,
\]

and the same formulas with capital letters for `D_Y,c_Y,l_Y`.
The original residue has the sign-times-sign character, so its density
contains `1/delta`, where `delta^2=D_X D_Y`.

## 2. A normal coordinate with the full Jacobian retained

Take `h=F` and `u=(r+a)+i(R+b)`. Then

\[
r=\frac{u+(k+h/3)/u}{2}-a,\qquad
R=\frac{u-(k+h/3)/u}{2i}-b.
\]

With the four base coordinates held fixed,

\[
dr\wedge dR=\frac{i}{6u}\,du\wedge dh.
\]

The coefficient of the original double-pole form is consequently

\[
\frac{i\,rR}{6u\,\delta}\frac{du\wedge dh}{h^2}.
\]

Taking its residue means differentiating the entire density in `h`, including
`delta`. Since `r_h=1/(6u)` and `R_h=i/(6u)`, the resulting relative one-form is

\[
\omega=\frac{C(u)}{\delta}\,du,
\]

\[
C(u)=\frac{i}{36u^2}\left[
R+ir-\frac{rR}{2}\left(\frac{l_X-54r}{D_X}
  +i\frac{l_Y-54R}{D_Y}\right)\right].
\]

This uses an actual normal coordinate, not an assumed fixed branch or a
polynomial-source approximation.

For a polynomial model put `y=u^2 delta` and, at `h=0`, define

\[
\rho=ur=\frac{u^2+k}{2}-au,\qquad
\upsilon=uR=\frac{u^2-k}{2i}-bu,
\]

\[
d_X=c_Xu^2+l_Xu\rho-27\rho^2,\qquad
d_Y=c_Yu^2+l_Yu\upsilon-27\upsilon^2,\qquad H=d_Xd_Y.
\]

Thus `y^2=H(u)`, with `deg H=8` and constant leading coefficient `-729/16`.
The exact residue has the compact polynomial numerator

\[
\omega=\frac{B(u)}{H(u)y}\,du,
\]

\[
B=\frac{i}{72}\left[
\upsilon(2c_Xu+l_X\rho)d_Y
 +i\rho d_X(2c_Yu+l_Y\upsilon)\right],\qquad\deg B\le8.
\]

`gauss_manin_circuit.py` checks the completed-square identity, normal-coordinate
Jacobian, and this full differentiated density algebraically. Its JSON output
stores the actual Gaussian-rational coefficient polynomials in the base
variables; it does not replace them by generic symbols with unproved values.

## 3. The projective chart has three independent base derivatives

On `p != 0`, divide by its weight:

\[
q\mapsto q/p^2,\quad P\mapsto P/p,\quad
Q\mapsto Q/p^2,\quad u\mapsto u/p^3.
\]

The affine residue has the radial factor `dp/p`. Contracting the Euler field
and taking the chart `p=1` therefore leaves the projective form

\[
dq\wedge dP\wedge dQ\wedge\omega.
\]

No `q`, `P`, or `Q` derivative may be discarded. The coefficient field for
the following circuit is `K=Q(i)(q,P,Q)`.

## 4. An explicit finite rank-six connection circuit

Write `H=sum h_j u^j`. On the squarefree locus, let `I` be the unique polynomial
of degree below eight satisfying

\[
H'I\equiv1\pmod H.
\]

It is computed by the inverse of multiplication by `H'` in `K[u]/(H)`, an
eight-dimensional algebra. Thus the entire construction can be kept as an
8-by-8 rational matrix circuit without expanding enormous fractions.

For any numerator `E` of degree at most fourteen, set

\[
V=-2EI\bmod H,\qquad
\mathcal R(E)=\frac{E+VH'/2}{H}-V'.
\]

The result is polynomial of degree at most six, and the exact identity is

\[
\frac{E}{Hy}\,du
=\frac{\mathcal R(E)}{y}\,du+d\left(\frac{V}{y}\right).
\]

Every numerical evaluation checks the equivalent polynomial identity
`E = H*(R(E)+V') - V*H'/2`.

Define

\[
c_1=-\frac{h_7}{2h_8},\quad
c_2=\frac{3h_7^2}{8h_8^2}-\frac{h_6}{2h_8},\quad
c_3=-\frac{5h_7^3}{16h_8^3}
  +\frac{3h_7h_6}{4h_8^2}-\frac{h_5}{2h_8}.
\]

These are the first three coefficients of the reciprocal square-root expansion
at infinity. A polynomial numerator `sum a_j u^j` of degree at most six has
zero residues at infinity exactly when

\[
a_3+c_1a_4+c_2a_5+c_3a_6=0.
\]

A basis for the six-dimensional projective, second-kind quotient is

\[
f=(1,u,u^2,u^4-c_1u^3,u^5-c_2u^3,u^6-c_3u^3),
\qquad \beta_j=f_j\,du/y.
\]

For each of the three base variables `b=q,P,Q`, column `j` of the connection
matrix is the basis coefficient vector of

\[
\mathcal R(-f_j H_b/2)+(f_j)_b.
\]

The last term is included: the chosen basis varies with the base. Source `v`
is the coefficient vector of `R(B)`. Both source and all connection columns
satisfy the zero-residue relation.

A sufficient rational primitive in this module would solve

\[
v=(\partial_q+M_q)U+(\partial_P+M_P)V+(\partial_Q+M_Q)W,
\qquad U,V,W\in K^6.
\]

This is an explicit source and connection, not a theorem assuming that the
unknown three vectors exist.

## 5. Exact verification and the bounded primitive search

The implementation evaluated the complete circuit at 100 smooth points over
`F_101`, with `i=10`. At every point it verified all Hermite polynomial
identities and the zero-residue conditions for the source and all eighteen
connection columns.

A separate calculation over `Q(i)` at `(q,P,Q)=(0,1,0)` computes all three
connection matrices and the source

\[
\left(
\frac{6475(1-i)}{76196},
\frac{17475i}{38098},
-\frac{289187(1+i)}{457176},
-\frac{364113(1-i)}{609568},
-\frac{285039i}{609568},
\frac{452709(1+i)}{4876544}
\right).
\]

Every one of the 108 exact connection entries and six source entries reduces
to the independently computed finite-field values. The exact replay also
differentiates the inverse/reduction circuit and checks all three connection
curvature matrices at this point.

The search permitted all eighteen components of `U,V,W`. It tried polynomial
numerators of total base degree at most 0, 1, 2, and 3, with common denominator
either `1` or `Delta=Norm(H')`. For a `Delta^-m` denominator the implemented
equation includes the term `-m*(partial_b log Delta)*Id`; it does not freeze
the denominator under differentiation.

The logarithmic derivative is computed by

\[
\partial_b\log\Delta
=\operatorname{Tr}_{K[u]/H}\left(
\frac{H_b'}{H'}-\frac{H''H_b}{(H')^2}\right).
\]

Both degree-three systems have 360 unknown constants. They reached coefficient
rank 360 and augmented rank 361 by the 61st sampled point. Thus this compact
ansatz did not produce a primitive. The lower-degree trials also failed; their
full results are retained in `gauss_manin_audit.json`. These are exact bounded
linear-algebra calculations, not Lean kernel checks or an unrestricted no-go.

## 6. A rigorous obstruction to the fiber-only shortcut

On the exact fiber `p=P=1`, `q=Q=0`, put `z=r+1`, `w=R+1`. The conic equation
is `3(z^2+w^2)=4`, parametrized by

\[
z=\frac{u+4/(3u)}2,\qquad
w=\frac{u-4/(3u)}{2i}.
\]

The squared branch factor is

\[
D=-\frac{
(3u^2-6u+4)(3u^2-6iu-4)
(27u^2-46u+36)(27u^2-46iu-36)}{144u^4}.
\]

The degree-eight numerator is squarefree, checked over `Q(i)`. Hence the
sign-times-sign double cover has genus three. The actual source above becomes

\[
C=-\frac{i(4+4i)\bigl(81u^4-492iu^2+(-400+400i)u+144\bigr)}{
216u^2(27u^2-46u+36)(27u^2-46iu-36)}.
\]

If `C du/delta` were exact in `C(u,delta)`, average a primitive into its odd
character. It must have the form `S(u)/delta`, with `S` rational, and satisfy

\[
S'-\frac{D'}{2D}S=C.
\]

The following pole bounds are exhaustive.

* At an ordinary nonzero finite point, a pole of `S` would differentiate into
  a pole of larger order, absent on the right side.
* At a branch point, `-D'/(2D)` has residue `-1/2` and `C` has at most a
  simple pole. A pole of `S` of order `m>=1` would produce a nonzero leading
  coefficient `-m-1/2` of order `m+1`, impossible.
* At zero, `D` has a fourth-order pole, so the connection coefficient is
  `2/u+O(1)`; `C` has at most a double pole. A pole of `S` of order greater
  than two would have nonzero leading coefficient `2-m` and is impossible.
* At infinity, the connection coefficient is `-2/u+O(u^-2)` and
  `C=O(u^-2)`. Polynomial growth of `S` greater than `u^2` has a nonzero
  leading coefficient `m-2` and is impossible.

Therefore every possible rational primitive is covered by

\[
S=(a_0+a_1u+a_2u^2+a_3u^3+a_4u^4)/u^2.
\]

Clearing the rational ODE yields a 12-by-5 Gaussian-integer coefficient matrix.
The augmented matrix has a six-by-six minor using zero-based rows
`[0,1,2,3,4,7]` whose determinant is **3 modulo 5**, with `i` mapped to 2.
Since the coefficient matrix has only five columns, this proves inconsistency
over `Q(i)` and over `C`. The full matrix and minor are retained in
`conic_primitive_audit.json`, generated by its companion script.

Normalized trace commutes with derivations in characteristic zero. A primitive
in any finite algebraic extension would therefore descend to one in
`C(u,delta)`. The obstruction also excludes such an algebraic fiber-only
primitive.

This conclusion is deliberately limited to the displayed fiber and to
integration in its curve direction. It does not discard base derivatives,
rule out a primitive with new support, or contradict Ben Diamond's existence
argument.

## 7. The remaining constructive obstacle and PR219

The rank-six circuit treats differentials of the second kind on the complete
generic curve. A full rational primitive may instead have simple poles at
new moving points. Their nonzero residues add logarithmic classes, so the
rank-six PDE is a sufficient ansatz rather than an equivalence for all possible
denominators.

PR219's genus-four norm contacts and moving-point currents address precisely
this kind of additional support. The literal formula in Chapter 2, equation
(2.8.4), supplies an exact source only after its normalized trace triple equals
the requested source as a rational-function identity. It also requires a
closed algebraic parameter one-form; arbitrary coefficient multipliers need
not preserve closedness. The chapter explicitly separates that obligation
from cohomological spanning and from chart corrections.

The most specific next step is to construct the actual moving-point finite
algebra for this target and its closed residue forms, adjoin their logarithmic
classes to the circuit above, and solve the corrected source equation with
all base derivatives. The new 48-dimensional geometric span is useful input,
but does not itself supply that missing exact source identity.

## Reproduction

Use an environment with SymPy installed:

```sh
python conic_primitive_audit.py
python gauss_manin_circuit.py
python gauss_manin_exact_fiber.py
```

The circuit script regenerates `gauss_manin_circuit.json` if absent, otherwise
reuses its exact sparse coefficient data. Remove only that generated file to
force a full symbolic rebuild. All calculations are local; no remote
repository was mutated.
