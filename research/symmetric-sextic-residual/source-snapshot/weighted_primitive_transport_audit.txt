1. INDEPENDENT SOURCE AND SCALE CHECK

The full six-coordinate chart, in the specified order (a,lambda,b,c,d,v), is

  X=(a(1+v),a(1-v),a*b),
  Y=(a(c+lambda*v),a(c-lambda*v),a*d).

Its Jacobian determinant is +4*a^5*v. This was independently recomputed as
an exact symbolic 6-by-6 determinant. The original numerator pulls back to
a^6*P(v^2), and the equation pulls back to a^6*f(v^2), with

  P(u)=b*d*(1-u)*(c^2-lambda^2*u),
  f=rawWeighted.

The projective volume is contraction of the ambient six-volume with the
radial field. That radial field is a*d/da in this chart, so the projective
volume pulls back to

  4*a^6*v d(lambda) wedge db wedge dc wedge dd wedge dv.

Thus the a powers cancel in A*Omega/F^2, giving precisely the affine source
A_H=4*v*P(v^2) over H=f(v^2). Changing to u=v^2 gives

  d(lambda) wedge [2*P(u) db wedge dc wedge dd wedge du / f(u)^2].

With G=f/ell and R=2*P/ell^2 this is also d(lambda) wedge R*Omega/G^2.
The ell^-2 factor is essential before cancellation. The verified identity
monic_residue_coefficient proves that the resulting coefficient is

  rho=2*(f_u*P_u-f_uu*P)/f_u^3.

The independent script weighted_primitive_transport_source_audit.py checks
the determinant, both homogeneous bindings, the leading coefficient ell,
and the normal-residue scalar identity. Its JSON receipt binds the two Lean
source hashes. These symbolic checks are distinct from the Lean proofs.
SymmetricAffineJacobian now additionally proves the full polynomial Jacobian
binding, determinant +4*a^5*v, both protected homogeneous scale identities,
and the complete rational coefficient identity after multiplication by
radial scale a. Its exact_projective_source_coefficient theorem proves
A_pullback*a*det(J)/F_pullback^2 = chartSource/chartEquation^2 for a != 0.
Thus these determinant and scale identities are no longer merely external
checks. The interpretation as a pullback of alternating differential forms
still uses the corresponding determinant and contraction definitions.

2. SIGN AND DIFFERENTIAL CONVENTIONS

Write the relative two-form as

  beta=beta_0 dc wedge dd - beta_1 db wedge dd + beta_2 db wedge dc.

Its relative differential has coefficient sum E_i(beta_i) in db wedge dc
wedge dd. The module requires that sum to equal the displayed positive rho.
Then gamma=-d(lambda) wedge beta satisfies d(gamma)=d(lambda) wedge d_rel beta.
Lambda dependence of beta causes no extra term because it is killed by the
second d(lambda) factor.

The repository residue-clearing convention is

  r + sum [E_i(w_i)-w_i E_i(g)/g] = 0.

Here g=H_v=2*v*f_u, and the polynomial derivative calculation gives

  r=(A_H,v*g-A_H*g_v)/g^2 = g*rho.

Therefore the actual clearing current is

  w=(0,-g*beta_0,-g*beta_1,-g*beta_2),

ordered (lambda,b,c,d). Each summand is -g*E_i(beta_i) by the product rule.
This is exactly the statement of relativeCurrent_equation and, with the
actual quotient tangents, canonical_current_equation.

If Omega=d(lambda) wedge db wedge dc wedge dd, the absolute primitive is
-i_w(Omega)/g. The minus sign is part of this clearing convention. The vector
w should not be described as the positive contraction coefficients of gamma.

For a Poincare-residue convention placing dG/G first, the local simple
residue in the normal-last coordinate has the opposite sign to the displayed
rho. A geometry-to-primitive theorem must fix that convention or negate its
two-form once. This does not affect an exactness/vanishing assertion. The
transport's required input and resulting target have no sign ambiguity:
they are the explicit equalities proved in Lean.

3. CANONICAL OPERATORS, NOT ASSUMED COMPATIBILITIES

chartTangent is the repository's residueTangent, constructed from the actual
quotient map and normal derivative. chartTangent_squareLift proves its action
on every pulled-back weighted polynomial:

  E_i(S(p)) = S(p_i)-S(f_i)/S(f_u)*S(p_u).

Here S denotes evaluation after u=v^2 into the residue function field. Thus

normal derivative g is nonzero.

4. WHAT THE CLEARING ENDPOINT PROVES

For an actual quotient domain B and its fraction field K, and any finite
extension L/K, the endpoint takes beta in L with

  sum_(i:Fin3) extendFiniteDerivation(E_(i+1))(beta_i) = image(rho).

It constructs w explicitly, proves its canonical residue equation, descends
it by normalized trace, and applies the repository's legal clearing theorem.
It produces polynomials D,Nv,N_lambda,N_b,N_c,N_d,Q satisfying:

  D != 0,  IsRelPrime H D,
  Nv*H_v + sum N_i*H_i = A_H*D,
  residueDivNum(D,Nv,N) = H*Q,
  A_H*D^2 - D*(Nv*H_v+sum N_i*H_i) + H*residueDivNum = H^2*Q.

The denominator is nonzero in the quotient function field and therefore is
not a multiple of H. It is never obtained by dividing by the zero equation
in that field. Normalized trace needs no Galois assumption.

The only other endpoint inputs are the explicit quotient kernel/surjectivity,
domain/fraction-field structures, irreducibility of H, and H_v nonzero. The
parent is discharging these for the canonical quotient in SymmetricAffinePrime
and SymmetricAffineField. They are algebraic chart facts, not substitute
current-existence assumptions.

5. WHAT IS STILL MISSING

The endpoint does not prove that beta exists. Its relative derivative equation
is the concrete coefficient version of the missing relative rational de Rham
primitive. It is stronger in structure than an arbitrary five-coordinate game
solution because its lambda component is zero. The independently motivated
generic-fiber support/exactness theorem is what would supply this input.

The input is not a tautological renaming of the original verifier: the source
normalization, reflection pullback, density correction, explicit current,
finite trace, and legal denominator construction are all derived. Nonetheless
a conditional implication is not the requested unconditional certificate.

The final transport from these affine chart data to the protected six-variable
homogeneous certificate is also separate. It must preserve the polynomial
source and Jacobian factor just checked; no claim that this final transport
or the primitive-existence hypothesis is discharged is made here.
