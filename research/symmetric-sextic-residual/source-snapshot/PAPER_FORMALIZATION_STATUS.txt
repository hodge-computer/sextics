Start with SymmetricRegularCurrent.lean
--------------------------------------
Its main theorem is an exact equivalence:

  certificate_iff_regular_game_exists :
    HasCertificate F q A <-> RegularGameExists

RegularGameExists is defined explicitly in that file. Let P be the complex
polynomial ring in six variables and let L be its localization at all
nonzero polynomials relatively prime to F. Let K be the fraction field of P,
with the usual coordinate derivatives, and E the Euler derivation.
The remaining statement asks for

  v : Fin 6 -> L, r : L,
  E(v_i)=v_i for every i,
  A - v(F) + F*div(v) = F^2*r in K.

The forward theorem consumes an EXISTENCE PROOF for this statement. It does
not ask the caller to compute D, six polynomial numerators, Q, or any common
denominator. The reverse theorem constructs v_i=N_i/D and r=Q/D^2 from an
accepted certificate and proves their required Euler weights. Thus the
remaining existence condition is neither an added restriction nor a proof
that the original target holds.

New modules and their mathematical content
-----------------------------------------
1. SymmetricPrime.lean
   Proves primality of the exact protected F without geometric hypotheses.
   Regard F as monic in X0 and specialize X1=X2=Y2=0, Y1=1. The result is
   X0^6+Y0^6+1, Eisenstein over C[Y0] at Y0-I, because Y0^6+1 vanishes
   simply at I. Monic specialization gives irreducibility of the original
   F, and unique factorization gives Prime F.

2. TwoReflectionSectors.lean
   Proves the decomposition for commuting involutions into the two positive
   reflection sectors and the residual negative-negative sector. Its
   support-transfer conclusion is explicitly conditional on membership in
   a submodule; it does not assert a geometric coniveau theorem.

3. SignedSourceCharacters.lean
   Supplies exact polynomial and determinant-twisted linear actions for the
   target. The source has characters (+,-,-) and lies entirely in the
   residual sector. Identifying these actions with pullback on residue
   cohomology is a separate geometric statement.

4. MonodromyImageSpanning.lean
   Proves that the span of all translated images of a nonzero correspondence
   is invariant. Irreducibility and finite dimensionality then extract
   finitely many translates whose combined map surjects. It connects this
   to the previous Picard-Lefschetz linear-algebra criterion. Geometric
   monodromy and a nonzero algebraic correspondence remain explicit inputs.

5. PolynomialSupportedProjector.lean
   Constructs the polynomial projector from the minimal polynomial on the
   image subspace. If c=m(0), the polynomial is -c^(-1)*(m-c), with constant
   coefficient zero. The proof establishes its image, identity action on
   the subspace, idempotence, and equality with a compatible named projector.
   The adjoint corollaries prove injectivity of Gamma*Gamma-adjoint from
   surjectivity between finite-dimensional real or complex inner-product
   spaces. Algebraic cycles, polarization compatibility, rational structures,
   and geometric support are not asserted by this linear-algebra result.

6. AutomaticLegalClearing.lean
   A finite family in L has a common legal polynomial denominator. The
   quotient-rule bridge derives the normalized polynomial identities.
   A normal derivative not divisible by F is obtained from Euler's identity
   and the degree drop under differentiation. Upstream homogeneous
   normalization then supplies the exact verifier's degree conditions.

7. RegularEulerClearing.lean
   Starts with the ordinary rational game, retaining its regular remainder.
   Differentiation preserves regularity, and the proved Euler correction
   converts the game to normalized identities. It then invokes automatic
   legal clearing. No explicit polynomial certificate data is assumed.

8. CanonicalFractionCoordinates.lean
   Constructs the actual fraction field, injective polynomial embedding,
   and coordinate derivations. Proves that the derivations commute, using
   the polynomial commutator and the quotient rule. These are actual objects,
   not hypothetical coordinate data.

9. CertificateRegularCurrent.lean
   Proves the reverse direction, from Verify/HasCertificate to a regular
   homogeneous current with ordinary remainder. Its constructions and
   identities ensure that the final reduction is an equivalence.

10. SymmetricRegularCurrent.lean
    Supplies all polynomial, prime, coordinate, commutativity, and degree
    inputs for the exact target and proves the equivalence described above.

What remains from the paper
---------------------------
The geometric chain in docs/03b-weighted-threefolds.md and docs/01-certificates.md
is not present as proved Lean declarations in this checkout. Its main missing
parts construct the nonzero geometric correspondence from the Hodge-theoretic
argument; identify the supported projector as an algebraic cycle; specialize
and spread support in the family; pass through the pencil/quotient argument;
and compare divisor-supported residue classes with regular rational currents.

The required Hodge, cycle-class, relative-cohomology and comparison structures
are themselves not developed here. Classical choice permits nonconstructive
witness selection once existence is proved; it does not prove this remaining
existential by itself. See paper_dependency_audit.txt for the detailed source
audit and the distinction between polynomial facts and geometric conclusions.
