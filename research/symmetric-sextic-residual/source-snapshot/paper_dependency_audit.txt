Paper-to-Lean dependency audit for symmetric-sextic-residual, q = 1
================================================================

Scope and source snapshot
-------------------------
This audit reads the local sextics/docs/03b-weighted-threefolds.md,
especially sections 3B.6--3B.8, the prescribed-support theorem in
docs/01-certificates.md, and the specialization/relative-support lemmas in
docs/03-geometric-support.md section 3.6.  It concerns that nonconstructive
paper argument, not the later finite-algebra computations.

The protected conclusion is literally

  HodgeComputer.HasCertificate HodgeSubmission.F 1 HodgeSubmission.A

where HasCertificate F q A is defined in HodgeComputer/Verify.lean as

  exists D N Q, Verify F A F.totalDegree q D N Q.

Verify includes a nonzero homogeneous denominator, IsRelPrime F D, the
prescribed numerator degrees, and the full cleared polynomial identity

  A*D^2 - D*directional F N + F*divergenceNumerator D N = F^2*Q.

No explicit denominator bound or Q = 0 is part of this conclusion.
Classical choice could select a witness from a proved existence theorem;
it cannot supply the missing existence theorem itself.

One newly formalizable paper step
--------------------------------
Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.Charpoly.Basic provides the exact existing names

  LinearMap.aeval_self_charpoly
  LinearMap.isIntegral
  LinearMap.minpoly_coeff_zero_of_injective.

For a field K, a K-vector space V, a finite-dimensional subspace W, and an
endomorphism S with image in W and injective restriction to W, let m be
the minimal polynomial of S restricted to W.  Its constant term c is
nonzero.  Set

  p(t) = -c^(-1) * (m(t) - c).

Then p(0)=0, p(S) is the identity on W, its range is W, and it is
idempotent.  If a named map P has image in W and S*P=S, then p(S)=P.
These hypotheses do not assume the asserted polynomial equality.  They
are the precise finite-dimensional algebraic part of the paper's
supported-projector construction.  New code is in
PolynomialSupportedProjector.lean; consult its compile receipt for the
actual verification status.  The completed extended module passed the
pinned Lean 4.33.1 compiler.  All five main theorems' axiom audits list only
propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, with no sorryAx or added axioms.

The extension in the same module also proves

  PolynomialSupportedProjector.gram_injective_of_surjective
  PolynomialSupportedProjector.exists_polynomial_gram_identity
  PolynomialSupportedProjector.exists_polynomial_eq_projector_of_surjective_adjoint.

It uses the existing LinearMap.range_self_comp_adjoint and
LinearMap.injective_iff_surjective: for finite-dimensional real/complex
inner-product spaces, surjective Gamma has injective Gamma*adjoint Gamma.
The final corollary feeds this into the polynomial projector construction
on W; no separate injectivity hypothesis remains there.

The general polynomial result assumes only finite-dimensional W.  The
inner-product extension assumes finite-dimensional source E and target W;
the ambient H need not be finite-dimensional.  The separate whole-target
Gram identity assumes E and H finite-dimensional.

The geometry must still construct Gamma, identify S on W with its genuine
positive-inner-product Gram operator, and provide the rational structure.
Arbitrary surjectivity together with an arbitrary nondegenerate indefinite
pairing does not imply that Gamma*adjoint Gamma is invertible.  The paper
uses polarizations and Hodge compatibility, not just a bare bilinear form.

Missing geometric interfaces (not existing Lean declaration names)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The following mathematical statements have no corresponding definitions
and proved declarations in the inspected project/mathlib sources.  The
labels below name missing interfaces for planning; they are not axioms or
new declarations in any Lean file.

1. WeightedThreefold.supported_anti_projector:
   For each smooth invariant weighted sextic threefold T, there exist a
   divisor D and a rational algebraic cycle Z supported on D x D such that
   cycleClass Z = cycleClass ((Delta_T - Graph_sigma)/2).

   The old paper derives this using the containing Fano fourfold, rational
   Hodge-class algebraicity, Hodge loci, the Abel--Jacobi differential,
   monodromy, the polarization adjoint, cycle-class composition, Kunneth
   and Poincare duality, then specialization.  Finite multiplication ranks
   and the new polynomial calculation close only algebraic substeps.

2. SupportedProjector.specializes / SupportedProjector.relative_support:
   Very-general supported representatives specialize throughout a smooth
   projective family; a relative anti-projector concentrated in fiber
   cohomological degree 3 supports H^4 of the family on a divisor.

   The paper uses proper relative flag Hilbert schemes, flat cycle classes,
   finite cover/trace of relative correspondences, relative Kunneth and a
   Leray spectral-sequence identification.  Fiberwise coniveau alone is
   explicitly insufficient, as the paper itself explains.

3. TwoReflection.divisor_support:
   For the smooth even fourfold with the two commuting reflections,
   H^4(X,Q)^tau is contained in N^1 H^4(X,Q).

   The extra arguments include Fano quotient support for the positive
   sectors, the smooth blown-up pencil, finite-quotient cohomology,
   weak Lefschetz/duality, and restriction/closure/birational transfer of
   the minus-minus sector.  The elementary three-sector linear-algebra
   decomposition does not supply these geometric support statements.

4. Residue.support_to_certificate:
   If the residue of A*Omega/F^2 vanishes on X outside a divisor, there
   exist legal homogeneous D,N,Q satisfying Verify F A 6 1 D N Q.

   Chapter 1 proves more: a prescribed reduced support D allows denominator
   D at q=1.  Its stated inputs are mixed Hodge modules, strictness of
   projective direct image, the Hodge pole bound, algebraic de Rham/residue
   comparison, Bott vanishing, and the twisted Euler sequence.  None is a
   ready formal theorem in the inspected libraries.  For the final target
   existence it would suffice to prove the weaker support-to-certificate
   assertion without the prescribed-support pole bound.

At present there is no honest short Lean signature for items 1--4 using
already-defined project types: the relevant Hodge/cohomology/support and
cycle-class structures themselves are missing.  Writing an abstract Prop
called Supported and assuming one of these implications would only move
the central unproved assertion into a hypothesis.

Available algebraic bridge if a rational primitive is constructed
----------------------------------------------------------------
An alternative uses already-defined algebraic objects, without importing
the global Hodge framework.  In the fraction field K of the coordinate
ring of F=0, write rho for the polynomial quotient map, g=rho(d_0 F), and
E_i for the tangent derivations.  The missing concrete existence statement
is a tuple w : Fin 5 -> K, or such a tuple on a finite extension L/K, with

  (rho(d_0 A)*g - rho(A)*rho(d_0 d_0 F))/g^2
    + sum_i (E_i(w_i) - w_i*E_i(g)/g) = 0.

The exact existing theorem

  HoldoutConstruction.finite_residue_solution_clearing_canonical

in HodgeComputer/Constructions/HoldoutCover/FiniteResidueClearing.lean
proves legal polynomial clearing from this equation, under an irreducible
hypersurface/quotient-field presentation and the explicit derivation
compatibilities.  The exact existing theorem

  HoldoutConstruction.hasCertificate_of_normalized

in HomogeneousCertificate.lean then supplies homogeneous certificate data,
assuming Prime F, F and A homogeneous of degree 6, a legal denominator,
the two normalized identities, and a nonzero normal derivative modulo F.

The previous 51-module package did not prove Prime HodgeSubmission.F.
This algebraic hypothesis is now discharged by the separately compiled
proof_attempt8/SymmetricPrime.lean, with exact declarations

  SymmetricPrime.target_irreducible : Irreducible HodgeSubmission.F
  SymmetricPrime.target_prime :
    Prime (HodgeSubmission.F : HodgeComputer.AmbientPoly 5).

These theorems have no mathematical hypotheses and use no new axioms or
sorry.  The proof treats F as monic in X0, specializes the other variables
to obtain X0^6+Y0^6+1, and applies Eisenstein at Y0-I.  Thus no geometric
smoothness-to-irreducibility theorem is needed for this algebraic bridge.

The current packet's CurrentVerifierBridge.verify_of_residue_primitive
and verify_of_affine_residue_primitive are other proved algebraic adapters.
They require the actual primitive equation and an explicit legal
homogeneous clearing.  They do not manufacture the primitive from smoothness
or symmetry alone.

Thus neither the existing library nor the polynomial projector lemma alone
closes the protected HasCertificate theorem.  The nonconstructive argument
is a mathematical existence proof, but translating its geometric support
and residue-comparison chain remains substantive formal development.

Current algebraic endpoint and surviving obligation
---------------------------------------------------
The new AutomaticLegalClearing module has compiled successfully.  Its
RegularRing F is the localization at the multiplicative set

  { D : AmbientPoly 5 | D != 0 and IsRelPrime F D }.

Therefore a tuple in this ring is already regular along F=0.  The new
theorems common_legal_representatives, normalized_data_of_regular_current,
exists_normal_chart and hasCertificate_of_regular_current construct the
common legal denominator and homogeneous certificate automatically.  The
application no longer needs to supply D, N, Q, a clearing equality or a
normal chart.  The target Prime hypothesis is now supplied by SymmetricPrime.

RegularEulerClearing also compiled successfully, extending that endpoint by
an Euler correction.  Its exact final theorem is

  RegularEulerClearing.hasCertificate_of_exists_regular_homogeneous_game.

Its precise remaining mathematical input is the following existence
statement, where rho = regularMap c F and delta_i are the compatible
coordinate derivations:

  exists (v : Fin 6 -> RegularRing F) (r : RegularRing F),
    (forall i, Euler(rho(v_i)) = rho(v_i)) and
    c.embed A - sum_i rho(v_i)*delta_i(c.embed F)
      + c.embed F * sum_i delta_i(rho(v_i))
        = (c.embed F)^2 * rho(r).

Here Euler(f) = sum_i c.embed(X_i)*delta_i(f).  This is Euler weight one
for the vector-field components, with a regular remainder r.  The
coefficient coordinates and commuting derivations are separate algebraic
setup, not geometric support assertions.

This existential is not proved merely by the projector polynomial, the
monodromy linear algebra, smoothness, irreducibility, or reflection
identities.  It must come from an actual regular current construction, or
from formalizing the paper's geometric support and residue-comparison chain.
An unrestricted rational vector field is insufficient: a pole on F can
create a spurious Euler solution.  The localization membership is essential.
