## 1. Immutable mathematical target

Coordinates are ordered

\[
z=(X_0,X_1,X_2,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2).
\]

\[
F=\sum_{i=0}^2(X_i^6+Y_i^6)
+\frac12\left(X_0^3X_1Y_0Y_1+X_1^3X_2Y_1Y_2+X_2^3X_0Y_2Y_0\right),
\]
\[
A=X_0^2X_1^2X_2^3Y_0^2Y_1^2Y_2,\qquad q=2.
\]

We require a nonzero homogeneous polynomial D of degree d, relatively prime to F over **C**, six homogeneous N_i of degree d+7 (zero components permitted), and an unrestricted polynomial Q such that

\[
\boxed{AD^2-2D\sum_{i=0}^5N_iF_i
+F\left(D\sum_{i=0}^5\partial_iN_i-\sum_{i=0}^5N_i\partial_iD\right)=F^3Q.}
\]

There is no imposed denominator-degree bound, pole-support restriction, rational-coefficient-only restriction, or Q=0 condition. In a homogeneous nonzero quotient, its degree is forced by the identity; that does not authorize deleting the quotient. A Q-coefficient candidate must still satisfy complex denominator legality. A closed existence proof is allowed, but conditional assembly and an admitted lemma are not that proof.

The reports preserve the original nontrivial residue character, actual source, full moving derivatives, Piola/Jacobian densities, normalized finite descent, and regular normal remainder. Every construction in the ledger must be understood with its own coordinate names; its auxiliary “A”, “D”, “q”, “p”, or “S” need not mean the original objects above.

See [the bundled exact target](formal/CycleQ2Workspace.lean), [the bundled verifier](formal/HodgeComputer/Verify.lean), and [the shared source account](research/sources/pro20_context.md).

## 2. Meaning of the outcome labels

| Label | What it records | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Partial / reusable | A construction, source reduction, channel cancellation or reconstruction identity reported as exact | Full source closure |
| Auxiliary integration success | A primitive for the explicitly different auxiliary equation/source class | Transport of the actual original source into that class |
| Scoped exclusion reported | The cited report gives a complete obstruction with specified field, directions, support or coefficient family | Unrestricted nonexistence |
| Finite-field / finite-space failure | The stated matrix/profile has no candidate, with the stated evidence | Every denominator, every degree, or automatically characteristic zero |
| Formal / finite-jet success | A local coefficient construction with its moving derivatives retained | Global rationality or convergence to a rational function |
| Conditional reconstruction | A supplied solution would give the desired current or certificate | Existence of the supplied solution |
| Software / representation success | Compiler, circuit, factor cancellation, parser or arithmetic checks worked | A mathematical certificate |
| Withdrawn / disputed | The record identifies an error, incomplete proof link or incompatible status | Permission to use the stronger claim as established |

**Evidence policy:** “reported” means established according to the cited archived account, at the scope that account states. The handoff does not independently validate those proofs. Raw source snapshots and archived receipts are included where available. Editorial context extracts are clearly distinguished from original report bytes.

### A — Source-preserving reductions and reusable constructions

#### A01. Exact physical-to-cubic source transport

**Recorded outcome:** PARTIAL / REUSABLE.

**What worked / was obtained.** The Pro20 record fixes R=X2², P=Y0Y1/(X0X1), U=Y1³/X1³ and Z=Y2/X2, with F=(R³/U)G. It carries the actual third-pole density, the coefficient functions of the plane cubic, and moving-root derivatives. The Weierstrass differential normalization is dx/(2y)=db/G_a, not the earlier factor-three convention.

**What did not close / remains missing.** No primitive follows just from presenting the source on an elliptic cubic. Physical transport and the nontrivial residue character must survive every subsequent step.

**Exact scope and reuse warning.** This is the common source binding used by several distinct architectures; it is not a certificate.

**Evidence:** [S01](research/sources/pro20_context.md).

**Recorded outcome:** PARTIAL / CONDITIONAL.

A separate fixed-sextic q=1 companion reduction gives an exact formula converting a solution of its remaining q=1 equation into the desired q=2 current.

**What did not close / remains missing.** The retained R2reg or E_tot source is not integrated. Multiplying a previously accepted q=1 primitive by a source factor does not by itself supply the correct q=2 character or all derivative terms.

**Exact scope and reuse warning.** The bridge is useful reconstruction, not an existence theorem for its unsolved input.

## 7. Do-not-repeat checklist

Before a new computation, record the exact target hash, coordinate/basis convention, source identity, allowed current module, denominator factors and powers, numerator support, field/prime, all active derivatives, and intended reconstruction. Compare that signature with the cited packet—not only the route’s title.

Do not present the following as a new solve: rebuilding unchanged circuits; repeating the same excluded profile; matching only moments; producing more local Taylor coefficients after a directional rationality obstruction; suppressing a boundary conservation law; changing the source or its character; erasing a nonzero F² remainder by renaming Q; demonstrating only generic invertibility; or compiling a theorem whose essential existence premise is admitted.

A failed candidate is a useful exact rejection when checked against the original identity. It is not an unrestricted impossibility theorem. A finite-field successful fit is only a discovery step until coefficients are reconstructed and a characteristic-zero identity is checked. A timeout, memory cap or missing compiler supplies no mathematical sign in either direction.

For a claimed positive conclusion, preserve the exact six-variable identity, all six numerator components, nonzero homogeneous D, complex coprimality, correct degrees, and the full Q. For a Lean conclusion, preserve the protected statement, actual toolchain/repository/dependency pins, successful compiler output, kernel replay and axiom audit. An accepted project receipt is a further separate event.
