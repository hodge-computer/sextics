transversality E(S), source numerator and every denominator remain units
at that lifted point. The finite residue calculation is therefore the
reduction of a well-defined characteristic-zero algebraic residue; the
value57 proves that residue is nonzero. It is not a positive-characteristic
integration argument.

More intrinsically, after adjoining a local square root of U, put
\(W=U^{9/2}\). Then E(W)=9W and a solution would make

\[
d_E(WF)=\frac{Wg}{E(S)}\,dS.
\]

On the smooth transverse chart this differential has a rational finite-jet
relative residue along S=0. Its nonzero regular specialization shows that
the generic divisorial residue is nonzero. This avoids assuming a putative
rational function is defined on a particular specially chosen whole orbit.
A rational exact differential has zero residue, whatever its pole order.
The harmless local normalization by W(1) is a nonzero unit.

Further finite extension cannot solve this same linear equation: normalized
field trace commutes with its uniquely extended characteristic-zero
derivation and preserves its known source, and would give a solution in
the original field.
