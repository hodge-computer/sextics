# Worker A — Generic 13-dimensional connection for cycle-q2

## 1. Exact generic genus-four curve

The plane-section geometry can be reconstructed algebraically from the immutable cycle-q2 cubic.

Set

\[
\begin{aligned}
L&=P^6+U^2,& \mu&=\frac{PU^2}{2},\\
D_c&=U^4+U^2,& s&=\frac{PU^2}{6(P^6-U^2)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For

\[
G(a,b,Z)=La^3+\mu a^2b+D_cb^3+
\frac{ZU^2}{2}b^2+\frac{PUZ}{2}a+U(1+Z^6),
\]

the substitution \(a=sb\) gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{\frac{G(sb,b,Z)}{U}=C(Z,b;P,U)}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
C=Z^6+1+\alpha Zb^2+\beta Zb+\gamma b^3
}
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha&=\frac U2,\\
\beta&=\frac{P^2U^2}{12(P^6-U^2)},\\
\gamma&=U^3+U+
\frac{P^3U^5(2P^6-U^2)}
{108(P^6-U^2)^3}.
\end{aligned}
\]

I independently checked this identity symbolically over \(\mathbb Q(P,U)\), with zero remainder.

Its Newton polygon has vertices \((0,0),(6,0),(0,3)\), with four interior lattice points. When the curve is nondegenerate with respect to this polygon, its smooth compactification has genus four. Thus its projective de Rham cohomology has dimension eight.

The proposed 13-dimensional system supplements those eight compact classes with five independent residue classes from six additional punctures. The distinction matters: the connection for the compact curve and the connection for its punctured counterpart are not interchangeable.
