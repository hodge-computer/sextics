# Residue transport and moving cubic maps

These modules express residue transport, normal changes, first variations and coordinate independence. The Piola and moving-map identities follow `hodgepodgeconjecture`'s [cyclic cubic construction](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/pull/165), building on `fqlx` and the Hodge Computer collaborators' residue framework. The original construction acknowledges OpenAI Codex assistance. The balanced-source normal identity was prepared for `benediamond` with OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex assistance for the mixed-polar construction. The scalar source-change identity records the normal correction in `fqlx`'s [shear construction](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/discussions/58#discussioncomment-19111681), prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance.

Import `HodgeComputer.Constructions.ResidueTransport`, or an individual module separately.

| Module | Main statements | Application obligations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Density` | `CycleResidueTransport.change_density`, `piola_pullback` | Nonzero normal derivatives; inverse coordinate matrices; the chain rule and equality of mixed partials. The multiplier is the ratio of normal derivatives times the coordinate determinant. |
| `NormalChange` | `ResidueNormalChange.paired_normal_change`, `normal_source_gauge`, `normal_source_gauge_of_constant` | The paired-normal identity requires nonzero normals, the specified tangent derivatives and `D B = 0`; its correction is `-B * p * q² / (p * f + q * g)`. For `g ≠ 0`, the scalar identity is `D ((h - c*g)/g) = D (h/g) - D c`; the constant version assumes `D c = 0`. All three work over any field. |
| `MovingMap` | `CycleMovingMap.moving_weierstrass_jet`, `residue_source_from_jet`, `moving_weierstrass_source` | The cubic equation, invariant differential identity, and compatible first variations. The jet form applies at a fixed parameter value without assuming a parameter derivation on the specialized field. |
| `FirstJets` | `ResidueFirstJets.ring_chain`, `invariant_from_cleared`, `curve_from_syzygy`, `curve_from_syzygy27`, `squareZeroEval`, `curve_first_variation`, `differential_first_variation` | A polynomial syzygy and cleared differential identity, an inverse Hessian value, and compatible square-zero variations. The chain rule and evaluation at any square-zero element work over commutative rings. The integral syzygy variant uses explicit inverses of `3` and `27`; the normalized identities provide the inputs to `MovingMap`. |
| `CoordinateIndependence` | `ResidueCoordinateIndependence.injective_of_coordinate_derivations` | Characteristic zero, derivations fixing the coefficient field, and the identity matrix of coordinate derivatives. These imply injectivity of polynomial evaluation by degree descent. |

These identities transport a primitive; they do not construct one on an arbitrary elliptic family. The cyclic application supplies its own covariants, polynomial-curve incidence, normal, source, field embedding, and legal homogeneous clearing. Neither an arbitrary rational source nor a higher-pole target is automatically covered.

[Chapter 2, §2.9](../../../../../docs/02-explicit-constructions.md#29-moving-cubic-maps-and-jacobian-residue-transport) develops the mathematical identities. The accepted application is assembled in [PhysicalPrimitive.lean](../../../../../data/cycle-three/PhysicalPrimitive.lean). Its original modules remain unchanged as verification evidence. `FirstJets` extracts the generic parts of [CubicJetCalculus.lean](../../../../../data/cycle-three/CubicJetCalculus.lean); each application still proves its own cubic equation over the dual numbers and its cleared differential identity. The density and moving-map identities retain their original declaration namespaces. The normal change, jet calculus and coordinate criterion use `ResidueNormalChange`, `ResidueFirstJets` and `ResidueCoordinateIndependence`. Import the maintained transport modules instead of the original `MovingMap` and `ResidueTransport` copies, not alongside them.
